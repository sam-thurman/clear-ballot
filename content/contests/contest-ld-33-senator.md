## About this office

State legislators propose and pass state laws, set the state budget, and decide how taxes are collected to pay for state and local government.[^sos-offices] The state Senate has 49 members, one from each legislative district, elected to four-year terms, with about half the seats up for election every two years.[^sos-offices] The Senate's one exclusive duty is to confirm appointments made by the governor.[^sos-offices] Legislators must be registered voters of their district.[^sos-offices]
