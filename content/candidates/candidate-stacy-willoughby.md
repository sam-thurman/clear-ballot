## Background

In the Voters' Pamphlet, Stacy Willoughby describes herself as a first-time candidate.[^sos-pamphlet] She says she has worked 25 years in financial services, including as a financial advisor and a local bank branch manager.[^sos-pamphlet] She says she graduated from the Leadership Institute of North Central Washington and attended Central Washington University and Skagit Valley College.[^sos-pamphlet] She lists community roles as board president of Chelan Valley Hope, a nonprofit that helps families with rent, fuel, food, and eviction prevention.[^sos-pamphlet] She also lists herself as founder of Chelan Business Women Connect and a board member of the Lake Chelan Chamber of Commerce.[^sos-pamphlet] On her campaign website, Willoughby says she is a Washington native who grew up living around the world and has lived above Lake Chelan for almost a decade.[^site] The website says she lives there with her husband, Rick, and describes her as the daughter of a veteran.[^site]

## Positions

- **Taxes and budget:** In the Voters' Pamphlet, Willoughby says she will "champion a state budget that prioritizes tax fairness for rural families and small businesses."[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says she supports balanced budgets and an approach to "fund what works, cut waste."[^site]
- **Housing:** She says she will work for affordable housing "that serves each community."[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says she will work to increase housing supply, address predatory lending, cut permitting burdens, and invest in rural and workforce housing.[^site]
- **Trades and jobs:** She says she will promote trades training for young people.[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says she supports workforce training, small business support, and "locally driven economic development."[^site]
- **Schools:** Her website says she supports fully funding public schools and strong vocational and career-connected education programs.[^site]
- **Infrastructure:** Her website says she will push for long-term investments in roads, bridges, and broadband for rural and growing communities.[^site]
- **Care:** Her website lists "excellent care for kids and older adults" among her priorities.[^site]

## Affiliations and endorsements

Willoughby's party preference on the ballot is "Prefers Democratic Party."[^kc-sample-ballot] In the Voters' Pamphlet, she lists herself as board president of Chelan Valley Hope and a board member of the Lake Chelan Chamber of Commerce.[^sos-pamphlet] In the pamphlet, she self-reports endorsements from the 12th Legislative District Democrats, King County Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, AFT-Washington, and Sage Leaders Washington.[^sos-pamphlet] Her website's self-reported endorsements include Congresswoman Kim Schrier, Superintendent of Public Instruction Chris Reykdal, State Representatives Lisa Callan and Zach Hall, and the mayors of Snoqualmie, Leavenworth, and East Wenatchee.[^site-endorsements] The website lists more individual endorsements.[^site-endorsements]

## Record

Willoughby is not an incumbent.[^kc-sample-ballot] In the Voters' Pamphlet, she lists her elected experience as "First-time candidate."[^sos-pamphlet]

## Funding

According to Public Disclosure Commission filings, Willoughby's campaign had raised $166,693.07 and spent $159,676.13 as of October 13, 2026.[^finance] About 11.7 percent of contribution dollars came from small donors, meaning gifts of $100 or less plus unitemized gifts of $25 or less.[^finance] The largest contributors are the Washington State Democratic Central Committee ($26,973.48) and the House Democratic Campaign Committee ($15,900).[^finance] The filings list $15,730.79 in "small contributions," the lump sum campaigns report for unitemized gifts of $25 or less.[^finance] The top named individual contributors, at $2,400 each, are Paul Fischbach (retired) and Ashok Chadwaney (software engineer, Google).[^finance]
