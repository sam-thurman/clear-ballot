## Background

In the Voters' Pamphlet, Maggie Adams says she is a current member of IATSE Local 887, a stage employees' union, working as a wardrobe technician and costume designer.[^sos-pamphlet] She says she is also a member of Actors' Equity Association as an actor, musician, and teaching artist.[^sos-pamphlet] She lists past work as an executive business administrator at Microsoft, an executive assistant at Amazon, a caterer, and a lifeguard.[^sos-pamphlet] Under education, she lists Coastline Community College.[^sos-pamphlet] She says she received a Silver Award in Girl Scouts and volunteers with Habitat for Humanity, El Corazon, King County Parks, California State Parks, Seattle Public Schools, and the Washington State Thespian Society.[^sos-pamphlet] In the pamphlet, she describes herself as a working parent.[^sos-pamphlet] Her campaign mailing address is in Snoqualmie.[^site]

## Positions

- **Health care:** In the Voters' Pamphlet, Adams says she will work for health care access.[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says she considers comprehensive health care "a fundamental human right" and will work to bring it "to every corner of the state."[^site]
- **Wages and workers:** She says she will "work tirelessly for living wages."[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says everyone should be able to earn a living wage in a safe workplace "free from harassment and exploitation."[^site]
- **Taxes and cost of living:** She says she will work for "tax fairness and inflation relief."[^sos-pamphlet]
- **Environment:** She says she will work to protect parks, natural resources, and agricultural lands.[^sos-pamphlet] Her website says water conservation should be a priority and that communities and farmers should have top priority for water over data centers and out-of-state energy companies.[^site]
- **Education:** Her website says educators and support staff deserve good wages, excellent facilities, and safe working conditions.[^site]
- **Term limits:** Her website says she supports a constitutional amendment imposing term limits on elected offices and length-of-service limits on federal judges.[^site]
- **Voting age:** Her website says she supports amending the state constitution to lower the voting age to 16 in state and local elections.[^site]

## Affiliations and endorsements

Adams's party preference on the ballot is "Prefers Democratic Party."[^kc-sample-ballot] In the Voters' Pamphlet, she lists herself as a business representative and job steward for IATSE Local 887 and a delegate to the Martin Luther King Jr. County Labor Council.[^sos-pamphlet] She also lists membership in Actors' Equity Association.[^sos-pamphlet] Her pamphlet statement does not list any endorsements.[^sos-pamphlet] Her website has an "endorsed by" heading with no organizations named in its text.[^site]

## Record

Adams is not an incumbent.[^kc-sample-ballot] In the Voters' Pamphlet, she lists her elected experience as business representative and job steward for IATSE Local 887 and delegate to the Martin Luther King Jr. County Labor Council.[^sos-pamphlet] She does not list any public office.[^sos-pamphlet]
