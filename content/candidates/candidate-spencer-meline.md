## Background

In the Voters' Pamphlet, Spencer Meline says he is a small business owner and restaurateur in Leavenworth, Washington.[^sos-pamphlet] He says he built multiple businesses and has managed employees, payroll, budgets, permitting, and daily operations.[^sos-pamphlet] His campaign website says he was born and raised in Washington and is a husband and father of four.[^site] He says he completed coursework through Edmonds Community College while pursuing business and career opportunities.[^sos-pamphlet] In the pamphlet, he says his business partners with local organizations to provide meals to the community, and that he supports local schools, youth activities, churches, and community events.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he has spent years hiking, climbing, and fishing in the 8th District and that faith is central to his family.[^site]

## Positions

- **Cost of living:** In the Voters' Pamphlet, Meline says the cost of housing, groceries, fuel, insurance, and everyday life keeps rising and that he wants leadership focused on lowering costs.[^sos-pamphlet]
- **Small business and jobs:** Meline says he wants to strengthen small businesses and support family-wage jobs.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he wants to simplify Small Business Administration lending, create tax incentives for small-business hiring and investment, and simplify the tax code.[^site-priorities]
- **Housing:** His website says he wants to reduce regulatory barriers to housing construction, provide first-time homebuyer tax relief, and address large institutional investors buying single-family starter homes.[^site-priorities]
- **Border security:** Meline says he wants to keep the border secure.[^sos-pamphlet]
- **Public lands and wildfire:** Meline says he wants to responsibly manage public lands for future generations.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he supports expanded forest thinning, prescribed burning, and year-round wildfire prevention, and protecting hydropower.[^site-priorities]
- **Energy:** His website says he supports what it calls all-of-the-above energy, including hydropower, nuclear, renewables, natural gas, and domestic oil production.[^site-priorities]
- **Government reform:** His website says he supports congressional term limits, single-issue bills, a ban on individual stock trading by members of Congress, and enforceable limits on deficit growth.[^site-priorities]
- **Health care:** His website says he wants price transparency for non-emergency care, more competition among insurers and providers, and support for independent physicians.[^site-priorities]
- **Education:** His website says he wants to expand apprenticeships and technical education, teach practical skills such as financial literacy, and give parents access to information about what their children are taught.[^site-priorities]
- **Technology and AI:** His website says he supports federal privacy protections, retraining for workers affected by automation, and enforcing antitrust laws against dominant technology companies.[^site-priorities]

## Affiliations and endorsements

Meline's ballot listing says "Prefers Republican Party."[^kc-sample-ballot] In the Voters' Pamphlet, he lists self-reported endorsements from the King County Republican Party and the Snohomish County Republican Party.[^sos-pamphlet] His campaign website lists a self-reported endorsement from the Washington State Republican Party.[^site]

## Record

Under "Elected Experience" in the Voters' Pamphlet, Meline lists no prior elected office.[^sos-pamphlet]

## Funding

According to FEC filings, Meline's campaign raised $154,328.63 and spent $53,330.34 through September 30, 2026, and had $100,998.29 in cash on hand.[^finance] About 3.1 percent of receipts came from small donors, meaning unitemized individual contributions of $200 or less.[^finance] The largest contributor listed was the candidate himself, filed as Spencer Harrison Meline ($70,000.00) and Spencer Meline ($15,010.00), with occupation listed as self-employed business owner.[^finance] Other top contributors were Meline Services ($6,500.00, no employer or occupation filed), Laurel Christiansen ($5,000.00, retired), and Elizabeth Littrell ($4,000.00, accounts payable at Unified Door and Hardware).[^finance]
