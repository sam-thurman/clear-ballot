## Background

In the Voters' Pamphlet, Cliff Moon says he has sixty-three years of experience in the construction trades and fifty-four years in small business ownership.[^sos-pamphlet] He lists a Bachelor of Science in physical oceanography and a Master of Science in civil engineering, both from the University of Washington.[^sos-pamphlet] He says he has been a visiting faculty lecturer teaching entrepreneurship and introduction to business at universities in Kazakhstan, India, and the University of Washington Bothell.[^sos-pamphlet] His campaign website says he has lived in Legislative District 1 for 50 years and is raising his family in Lake Forest Park.[^site] The website says he worked as a journeyman steelworker while attending the University of Washington.[^site] Under elected experience, the pamphlet lists precinct committee officer for Legislative District 1.[^sos-pamphlet]

## Positions

- **Cost of living and taxes:** In the pamphlet, Moon says the cost of gas, groceries, and utility fees eats away at income and that layers of taxes are driving up costs.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he will fight to reduce the tax burden on working families.[^site-issues]
- **Public safety:** In the pamphlet, Moon says he supports law enforcement and public safety.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he will stand with officers and restore what it calls accountability in the courts.[^site-issues]
- **Family and parental rights:** In the pamphlet, Moon says parents should raise their children and schools should focus on academics.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he will defend parental rights in education and supports a curriculum centered on reading, math, science, and civics.[^site-issues]
- **Small business:** His website says he will cut red tape and reduce the business and occupation tax burden on small operators.[^site-issues] The business and occupation tax is the state's tax on gross business receipts.[^site-issues]
- **Immigration:** In the pamphlet, Moon says he supports legal immigration.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he supports an orderly, lawful immigration system.[^site-issues]
- **Infrastructure:** His website says he will prioritize investment in the district's roads, bridges, and transit connections.[^site-issues]
- **Government:** In the pamphlet, Moon says Olympia's "moral compass has been displaced" and common sense has been ignored.[^sos-pamphlet] His website says he will cut waste and demand accountability in state government.[^site-issues]

## Affiliations and endorsements

Moon's party preference on the ballot is "Prefers Republican Party".[^kc-sample-ballot] The King County Republican Party lists Moon among its endorsed candidates on its website.[^endorse-1] In the pamphlet, Moon also says he is endorsed by the King County Republican Party.[^sos-pamphlet] The pamphlet lists him as a precinct committee officer for Legislative District 1.[^sos-pamphlet] His campaign website lists no other organizational endorsements.[^site]

## Record

Under "Elected Experience", the pamphlet lists precinct committee officer for Legislative District 1.[^sos-pamphlet]
