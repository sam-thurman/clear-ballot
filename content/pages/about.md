Clear Ballot is a nonpartisan, volunteer-made guide to the King County, Washington ballot for the November 3, 2026 general election. It is not affiliated with any campaign, political committee, or party, and it does not endorse candidates or measures.

## What it tries to do

Ballot measures should be explained in plain English, with the arguments for and against, links to the real documents, and the impacts you may not be thinking about. Candidates' stated views, affiliations, funding, and public record should be laid out transparently from public filings. Every sentence of explanation on this site cites a source you can open yourself; the [Methodology](/methodology) page describes the rules and the sources that are allowed.

## What it does not do

It does not tell you how to vote. It does not cover county offices, lower-court judges, or city, school, and special-district measures; those are on your official ballot and in the [King County local voters' pamphlet](https://kingcounty.gov/en/dept/elections/election-information/2026/november-general). It does not store your address or track you. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies.

## Draft pages and corrections

Pages publish as soon as they are written so the information is available before ballots are due. A page that has not yet been checked by a human reviewer shows an "Unreviewed draft" banner at the top. If you spot an error, use the "Report an error" link at the bottom of the page; corrections are logged publicly on the [Corrections](/corrections) page.

## Useful official links

- [King County Elections](https://kingcounty.gov/en/dept/elections): ballot drop boxes, ballot status, and the local voters' pamphlet
- [VoteWA](https://voter.votewa.gov/): register to vote, update your address, and see your personalized state voters' guide
- [Washington Secretary of State Voters' Pamphlet](https://www.sos.wa.gov/elections/data-research/election-data-and-maps/election-results-and-voters-pamphlets/2026-general-election-voters-guide): the official statewide guide this site draws from

## Who makes it

Clear Ballot is built in the open by a volunteer. The code, the content files, and the fetched source documents are public at [github.com/sam-thurman/clear-ballot](https://github.com/sam-thurman/clear-ballot), where anyone can see exactly where each claim came from.
