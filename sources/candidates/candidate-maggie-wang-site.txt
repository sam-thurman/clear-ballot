My Story
Immigrant. Business Owner. Wife. Mother. Proud American.
After arriving from Taiwan in 1989, my husband and I achieved the American Dream through hard work. Today, that Dream is under siege by rising taxes, housing costs, and government overreach. We need leadership prioritizing your budget over bureaucratic expansion.
In Olympia, I will be your voice for Affordability, Public Safety, and Parental Rights . My focus is simple: lower taxes, safer neighborhoods, and an economy where small businesses thrive. Let us restore common-sense government together.
I am ready to fight for your family.

My Priorities
What I'll Fight For in Olympia
Stop Higher Taxes and Make Washington Affordable Again
Washington families are struggling with rising costs, higher taxes, inflation, housing prices, and expensive everyday necessities. I will fight to stop new tax increases and work to make our state more affordable for working families, seniors and small businesses.
Restore Responsible Government and Accountability
Government should work for the people — not special interests or political insiders. I support auditing state spending, cutting wasteful programs, increasing transparency, and returning to citizen-focused government where every voice matters.
Bring Common Sense Back to Schools and Government
I believe schools should focus on education, parental involvement, and preparing students for success. I oppose divisive political ideologies in government and education, and I support policies rooted in fairness, accountability, and American values.
Additional Policy Priorities
Practical, Common-Sense Legislation
Increase transparency in state data collection and reporting
Ensure government programs are balanced, accountable, and effective
Support law enforcement and first responders
Reduce unnecessary government spending
Pass practical, common-sense legislation that benefits Washington families
