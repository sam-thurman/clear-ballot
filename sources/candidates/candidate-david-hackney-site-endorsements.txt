ENDORSEMENTS

Governor Bob Ferguson

"David Hackney is a bold voice for progress who isn't afraid to take on powerful interests. Whether he is fighting for stronger protections for consumer or advocating for the fundamental civil rights of every Washingtonian, David is dedicated to building a state that works for everyone, not just those at the top. He understands that true progress requires dismantling systemic barriers and ensuring our economy is rooted in fairness. I am proud to endorse David Hackney for reelection. "

King County Executive Girmay Zahilay

"In the fight for a more equitable King County, David Hackney is a true ally. David understands that housing is a human right and that our public investments should prioritize people over profit. He’s been a consistent champion for the 11th District, pushing for transformative policies that strengthen the social safety net and lift up our working-class neighbors. David leads with empathy and a commitment to radical inclusion, and I am honored to stand with him for his reelection. "
