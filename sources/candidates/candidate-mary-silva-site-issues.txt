The Platform
A bold, America-first agenda to restore sovereignty, accountability, and freedom for every citizen.
Mary Silva · U.S. House of Representatives · WA CD-1
01
Tech Sovereignty, American Jobs & Workforce Development
Reshore Core Tech & Microchip Fabrication
Bring semiconductor manufacturing, IT, AI, and cybersecurity operations back to the U.S. to protect national security and safeguard American innovation.
H-1B Visa Reform & Local Hiring
Restructure the H-1B visa program to curb tech offshoring and incentivize corporations to prioritize local American talent over foreign labor.
Vocational Training & Reskilling
Expand apprenticeships, federal reskilling programs, and trade education as fast-track alternatives to four-year degrees, helping young workers earn high salaries sooner.
02
National Security & Counter-Espionage
Secure Borders & High-Level Clearances
Fortify border security and overhaul high-level security clearance procedures to combat foreign espionage.
Protect Critical Infrastructure
Investigate deep foreign penetration across America's economic, military, political, educational, and critical infrastructure systems.
9/11 Intelligence Transparency
Launch a full, transparent investigation into the involvement of foreign intelligence agencies in the September 11, 2001 attacks.
03
Defense & Intelligence Accountability
Audit the Pentagon
Execute a comprehensive, real audit of the Pentagon to stop waste and enforce fiscal responsibility.
Overhaul the $70 Billion Intelligence Community
End the “fail-up” culture and curb costly contractor handouts by prioritizing in-house government talent at a fraction of the cost to taxpayers.
04
Fiscal Integrity & Stop the Misuse of Taxpayer Funds
Crack Down on the Grift
Stop the misuse of taxpayer funds across all levels, from local program fraud to billion-dollar military contractor payouts.
Hold Wall Street Accountable
Demand a complete accounting of Wall Street executives who profited off taxpayer bailouts, paid themselves bonuses, and hid profits in offshore accounts.
05
Election Integrity & Civic Duty
100% Election Integrity
Require voter ID to guarantee secure, transparent, and trustworthy elections.
Midweek National Voting Holiday
Establish a midweek voting holiday to encourage civic participation and allow more citizens to observe and protect the voting process.
06
Health Freedom & Informed Consent
Medical Freedom & Informed Consent
Protect every individual’s fundamental right to make their own medical decisions without government mandates, digital tracking, or economic coercion.
Parental Rights in Healthcare
Guarantee parents retain absolute authority over all health, medical, and wellness decisions for their children.
Independent & Transparent Public Health
Strip corporate conflicts of interest from health agencies, hold manufacturers liable for product safety, and protect physician freedom to treat patients without government interference.
07
Digital Privacy & Surveillance
Dismantle Warrantless Mass Surveillance
End the intrusive domestic surveillance networks—such as widespread Flock license-plate camera systems—that monitor everyday law-abiding citizens without judicial oversight or transparency.
Ban Foreign-Linked & High-Risk Police Tech
Prohibit the integration of foreign-linked or compromised surveillance platforms into local law enforcement and 911 dispatch networks. End public contracts with high-risk technology entities to protect citizen privacy and national security.
Uphold Fourth Amendment Protections
Enforce strict federal privacy standards requiring explicit warrants before government agencies or third-party vendors can collect, store, or share citizens’ location data, video feeds, or personal communications.
Domestic-Only, Auditable Police Technology
All police tools and their related data must be fully domestically operated, controlled, and auditable by an independent agency—especially when abuse is suspected. Data must be stored securely and locally, or the technology must not be used.
Mary Silva
Candidate for U.S. House of Representatives · Washington’s 1st Congressional District
