Elected Experience
State Representative (2017-present)
Other Professional Experience
As Deputy Director at DESC (Downtown Emergency Service
Center), I help oversee a $150 million budget providing
supportive housing, integrated healthcare, and social
services to thousands who have experienced homelessness
in our region. My previous work includes supporting
domestic violence survivors and individuals living with
developmental disabilities.
Education
MPA, Evans School, University of Washington; BA,
Sociology, Rutgers University
Community Service
Past President, Washington Low Income Housing
Alliance; Former Member, Seattle University Institutional
Review Board; Human Right to Housing Award, National
Homelessness Law Center; Named one of Seattle’s most
influential people by Seattle Magazine and Seattle Met.
Statement
As your State Representative, I’ve led the fight to protect
abortion access, tackle climate change, and make housing
and healthcare affordable. I’ve championed landmark laws to
prevent rent gouging and enact historic tax fairness to fund
schools and childcare, lower costs for working families and
seniors, and cut small business taxes.
I’ve worked to reduce gun violence, address the fentanyl
crisis, and protect immigrant and LGBTQ+ neighbors from
hateful federal overreach. With your continued support,
I’ll keep advancing economic fairness and opportunity for
everyone. I’m voting no on all statewide initiatives and hope
our community unites against these harmful measures.
