Statement
To fight addiction, I passed Ricky’s Law, created the
Recovery Navigator Program, launched treatment programs
inside jails and ERs, and spearheaded the Lynnwood
Crisis Care Center. To combat domestic violence, I funded
training for judges, legal assistance for survivors, and victim
advocates who respond with police. I’ve championed
healthcare for immigrants and protections against ICE.
I’m completely pro-choice. I’m committed to tackling
affordability, education funding, homelessness, and gun
violence.
