Statement
Washington deserves Democratic leaders who deliver on
promises. My record shows I deliver.  As your Senator, I banned
ICE facemasks, capped rent increases, led the fight to block
AI-powered rent-raising schemes, ended sales taxes on
nonprescription medicines and hygiene products, expanded
low-income tax rebates for 460,000 additional households, and
cut taxes on small businesses. I designed and spearheaded
legislation that closed corporate tax loopholes, securing
hundreds of millions in new funding for healthcare, K–12 schools,
and childcare.
After knocking on thousands of doors, I’ve heard you loud
and clear: prices are out of control, and government needs to
be more responsible with your tax dollars. I’ll audit spending,
expand senior property-tax relief, stand up to ICE, and stop
Trump chaos in our state. We must confront the crisis on Aurora
with compassion and accountability by connecting people
to mental health and addiction treatment services, instead of
looking the other way.
I hope I’ve earned your vote for reelection.
