Steve Bergquist

Welcome and thank you for your support of my campaign to continue representing you in Olympia. I am a Public School Teacher at Lindbergh High School, a former Small Business Owner of Aces Tennis in Renton, a Union Member with the Renton Education Association and my family has been in this community for four generations.

I continue to be a certificated teacher with a seat on the House Education Committee and House Appropriations Committee where I actively support our students and families in Washington State.

I need your support as a volunteer and your kind donations to continue being a leader on education in our state legislature. Thank you for your donations and your time. Together we can make a difference in our community.
