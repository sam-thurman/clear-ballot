"I'm proud to endorse Judge Ian Birk for State Supreme Court. Judge Birk has the integrity, experience, and commitment to the rule of law we need on our court. Birk has earned the support of his judicial colleagues and organizations statewide, and he deserves our vote."
WA State Governor Bob Ferguson
"When Birk speaks, you can see the depth of his thinking. Especially around fairness, access to the courts, and the Court’s role in addressing racial inequities and mass incarceration. When he picked us and our questions apart, we thought, “this is exactly the person you’d want sitting on our State Supreme Court.” He’s no-nonsense, with zero interest in political games or in flattering the SECB. There is only the law, and its fair application for all people. He lives for this and treats it as a sacred responsibility. It was inspiring."
The Stranger’s 2026 Primary Election Endorsements
"Voters have an easy choice: Judge Birk is a thoughtful, experienced Court of Appeals judge who has written fair, deeply researched opinions on land use, transportation, and other urbanist issues...Vote Judge Birk."
https://www.theurbanist.org/2026-primary-election-endorsements/#state-supreme-court-position-4-ian-birk , The Urbanist
"I support Judge Birk because he has the appellate experience and the work ethic to do the job on Day 1. He has a keen legal mind, coupled with deep commitment to fairness and due process for all appearing in our courts. He truly cares about the people in the State of Washington."
WA State Supreme Court Justice Mary Yu (ret.)
“Judge Ian Birk has built a career standing up for individual people and ensuring the law works the way it is supposed to—fairly, consistently, and with integrity.
At a time when courts play a critical role in protecting our rights and upholding our laws, Washington needs justices who are independent, thoughtful, and committed to the people they serve. Ian Birk is exactly the kind of judge we need."
WA State Attorney General Nick Brown
“Throughout his career, Judge Birk has fought for accountability and equality under the law. He has taken on tough fights against powerful corporate interests, worked with marginalized communities, and stood up for our laws and constitution. We need him on the State Supreme Court.”
WA State Insurance Commissioner and former State Senator Patty Kuderer
“We need Justices who understand the full spectrum of State law, and the diverse communities east and west of the Cascades. Judge Birk knows this job is about securing the rights of all people, and ensuring every voice is heard and respected in our legal system.”
Spokane County Superior Court Judge Breean Beggs
“Judge Ian Birk has built a career grounded in integrity, respect for the rule of law, and access to justice for all. At a time when people want to trust that our institutions work for everyone, Ian brings the independence, thoughtfulness, and real-world experience we need on the Washington State Supreme Court. I’m proud to support him and confident he will serve the people of Washington with distinction.”
King Co. Executive Girmay Zahilay
"Judge Birk is superbly well qualified."
Becky Roe
"Appointed to the Washington State Court of Appeals in 2022, Birk brings decades of experience standing up for everyday Washingtonians and the pragmatic judgment needed to safeguard the Supreme Court and our values."
Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates General Election Endorsements
"Birk’s judicial philosophy centers on an independent and impartial judiciary that applies the law based on the facts, not politics or public pressure. He believes courts should provide equal access to justice regardless of a person’s wealth or background, and that judges have a responsibility to explain their decisions clearly, thoughtfully, and with respect for the real-world impact on families, workers, and communities."
Washington's Progressive Voters Guide
