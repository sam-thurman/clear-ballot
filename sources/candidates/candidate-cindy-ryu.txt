Statement
It has truly been an honor serving as your State Representative.
Now, I’m ready to bring my experience, proven results, and
progressive values to the State Senate. I will continue to fight
for a fairer tax system that asks the wealthiest to pay their share
while providing relief to working families, those on fixed incomes,
and helping small businesses succeed.
The contrast between the candidates in this race couldn’t be
more stark. My record is one of action and standing up for
Democratic values. I’ve championed legislation to protect
workers from unfair non-competes, increased affordable
housing—particularly for underrepresented communities —and
worked to bring broadband access to every corner of our state. I
will continue showing up, listening to you, and working tirelessly
on our biggest challenges like affordability, job creation, and
protecting our communities. That’s the difference in this race.
In the Senate, my priorities remain: Safeguarding the
environment, investing in our public schools, and ensuring
strong consumer protections against predatory practices. I’d be
honored to earn your vote again.
