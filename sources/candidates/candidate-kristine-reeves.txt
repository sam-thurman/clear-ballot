Your State Representative, 2017-2019 & 2023-present.

organizational development consulting. Previously served as

and Defense Sector at the Washington State Department

Statewide Veterans Constituency Coordinator and regional

Statement
An open door, open mind, and commitment to results.
That’s how I’ve served the 30th District, and I’m ready to
keep going. I’ve passed laws to protect homeless students,
expand access to nutritious food for seniors, mitigate wildfire
risk, and invest $100 million in local public safety. I founded
the bipartisan Veterans & Military Families Caucus, passed
the Wounded Warrior Shared Leave Act, and now chair
the House Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee,
focused on food, farms, and the people who put dinner
on our table. Endorsed: Congressman Adam Smith,
Mayors & City Leaders, Labor, Nurses, Teachers, Police, &
Environmentalists
