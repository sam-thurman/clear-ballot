Statement
Good policy comes from listening, not partisanship. I
bring an engineer’s problem-solving to Olympia. I’ll keep
fighting for great public schools, mental health care for
kids, workforce housing for the families who serve our
communities, and gun safety that saves lives. I’ll defend
reproductive freedom, fight for affordable childcare, protect
forests and clean air, and work for a tax code that doesn’t
squeeze working people. Endorsed: Educators, State Labor
Council, Grocery Workers, Firefighters, Nurses, Sierra Club,
Washington Conservation Action, Planned Parenthood,
Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Building Trades, Housing
Alliance, Congresswoman Kim Schrier, Mayors and Council
Members across the 5th LD.
