Statement
I’m running for Congress because too many Washington
families feel like the American Dream is slipping away. The
cost of housing, groceries, fuel, insurance, and everyday life
keeps rising. Small businesses are closing at alarming rates,
and many people feel ignored by career politicians who have
never experienced the challenges facing working families.
I’m honored to have earned the endorsements of both
the King County Republican Party and the Snohomish
County Republican Party. Their support reflects the growing
momentum behind our campaign and our message
of restoring opportunity for working families across
Washington.
I’m not a politician. I’m a husband, father, and small business
owner who built businesses from the ground up. I know what
it means to meet payroll, manage rising costs, and work long
hours to provide for a family.
I believe we need leadership focused on lowering costs,
strengthening small businesses, supporting family-wage
jobs, protecting our communities, keeping our border
secure, and responsibly managing our public lands for future
generations.
I’m running to bring a real-world perspective to Congress
and fight for the people who work hard, play by the rules,
and want a fair chance to build a better future.
