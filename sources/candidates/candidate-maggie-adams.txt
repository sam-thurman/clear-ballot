Statement
I am a working parent dedicated to ensuring access to
healthcare, education, and a healthy environment for
everyone in Washington State. As an elected Union Leader
responsible for negotiating and overseeing Collective
Bargaining Agreements I have worked to establish fair
wages, worker protections, and on the job safety standards.
If elected I will work tirelessly for living wages, healthcare
access, tax fairness and inflation relief as well as the
protection of our parks, natural resources and agricultural
lands. I look forward to the opportunity to serve District 12 as
your representative. Together we can build a better, brighter
Washington.
