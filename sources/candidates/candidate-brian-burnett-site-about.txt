About Brian 

Brian Burnett has spent his career protecting communities, managing public safety resources, and solving real
problems at the local level. As a former Chelan County Sheriff and State Representative for District 12, During
his first term in office, Brian brought steady, practical leadership to Olympia.

Brian’s Work in Olympia
