Re-Elect Mike Steele

Known for working across the aisle and putting people over politics, Mike has earned respect from Republicans, Democrats, and community leaders alike.

First elected in 2016, Mike is currently serving his fIfth term in the Washington State House of Representatives. He serves as the Ranking Member on the Capital Budget Committee. Mike also serves on the K-12 Education and the Consumer Protection and Business Committees.

A lifelong resident of North Central Washington, Mike grew up in the 12th Legislative District and attended local schools before dedicating his professional career to advocating for small businesses and strengthening local communities. Throughout his time in office, he has earned a reputation for collaborative leadership, bipartisan problem-solving, and delivering meaningful results for the people he represents.
