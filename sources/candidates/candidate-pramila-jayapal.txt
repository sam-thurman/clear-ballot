Statement
I am proud to represent Washington’s 7th district and
stand up for working people every single day. As your
Congresswoman, I’ve worked to protect our democracy,
raise wages, lower costs, build affordable housing, protect
abortion rights, and fight climate change.
I’m leading the fight for the Medicare for All Act to ensure
that universal healthcare is a right, not a privilege. I also
lead landmark legislation to make higher education tuition-
free, guarantee housing as a human right, expand workers’
rights, raise the federal minimum wage, implement humane
immigration reform, and establish an ultra-millionaire wealth
tax. I lead bipartisan bills that ban stock trading for Members
of Congress and protect Americans from government
spying. I’m doing everything possible to get money out of
politics and reform the Supreme Court.
I’ve been on the frontlines of holding Trump accountable,
including around the lawless ICE and CBP actions that have
kidnapped and disappeared our neighbors. I established the
Monopoly Busters Caucus to fight consolidation, corporate
greed, and price-gouging. I have authored a War Powers
Resolution to end Trump’s illegal war in Iran and worked
with Epstein survivors to get justice against predators and
pedophiles.
I’ve held over 130 town halls and helped thousands of
constituents, including veterans and seniors, secure over
$25 million in federal benefits. My office has brought
home more than $70 million in new federal investments for
housing, transportation, jobs, and public safety.
I’m proud to represent a diverse, innovative and
compassionate district. I have never taken corporate PAC
money because I fight for working people, not the billionaires
and giant corporations. I would be deeply honored to have
your vote.
Endorsements: Labor unions including Washington State
Labor Council, WFSE, UFCW 3000, NEA/WEA, and
Washington State Building & Construction Trades Council.
Planned Parenthood, Sierra Club, and the King County
Democrats.
