Statement
As a lifelong Washingtonian, I know our state’s incredible
potential. Unfortunately, Washington keeps going in the
wrong direction. We have among the highest gas, grocery
and housing prices and worst crime rates in the country.
Student test scores, attendance levels and college
enrollment rates are dropping. Meanwhile taxes keep
increasing, by billions each year.
It’s time for change. I’m running for reelection to reverse
these trends. I’ll fight for safer neighborhoods where laws are
enforced and criminals are apprehended, better schools that
prepare all students for college or career, and a stronger and
more affordable economy. Together, let’s fix Washington.
