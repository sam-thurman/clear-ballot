Statement
As a mother and small business owner, I cannot stand by
as our state continues down a destructive path. Families
are struggling with rising costs, including property taxes,
gas prices, and increasing utility bills, while government
spending continues to grow. We are not better off than we
were four years ago. Our children deserve the opportunity
to grow up and succeed in the state we know and love, but
that requires a serious change in direction. I am committed
to fighting for our future, supporting small businesses, and
restoring accountability in leadership that listens to and truly
represents the people.
