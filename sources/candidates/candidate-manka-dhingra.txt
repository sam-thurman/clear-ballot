Statement
We deserve leaders with a proven record of bringing people
together to solve tough problems. I have listened to our
community to deliver meaningful results. My work is guided
by science and data and rooted in collaboration, honesty,
and integrity. I have led efforts to strengthen public safety,
expand behavioral health care, protect reproductive freedom,
and defend civil rights.
We need bold leaders, committed to justice. I’ve worked
alongside survivors, law enforcement and community
organizations to improve victim services, strengthen
accountability, expand crisis response systems, and connect
people experiencing mental illness and addiction with
treatment.
Each of us has a role to play in the prosperity of our state.
We must continue advancing social equity, investing
responsibly in transportation, and empowering our children
to become the leaders we need to continue our tremendous
economic growth. Our state must remain a leader in
innovation and in creating opportunities for all.
With your vote, I would be honored to continue working to
protect our democracy, improve healthcare access, defend
workers, protect seniors, small businesses, and immigrants,
and fight for a safe and livable planet. Endorsements:
Planned Parenthood, One America Votes, Washington
Conservation Action, labor unions, nurses, and community
leaders across the Eastside.
