Statement
As your representative, I’m focused on building consensus
and strong communities. In my first session, I sponsored
and passed bipartisan bills to lower energy costs, prioritized
safety and congestion improvements along SR 18, and
supported reforms to fix our housing crisis. I’m committed
to supporting first responders, fully funding our schools,
helping small businesses thrive, and pushing for affordable
childcare and healthcare. I’d be honored to earn your vote.
