Priorities

Protecting What Matters Most

Tax Reform

More Local Control

Victim-Centered Justice

Supporting Small Businesses

Your rights, your property, and your role
as a parent should be protected—not
undermined by state government. Brian
believes families and individuals deserve
a strong voice in the decisions that affect
their homes, their children, and their
communities. In Olympia, he is focused
on defending personal freedoms,
protecting property rights, and ensuring
parents remain central in decisions
involving their children.

Taxpayers deserve a state government
that treats every dollar with respect.
Brian is committed to responsible
budgeting, greater accountability, and
making sure public dollars are focused
on core priorities that actually serve
Washington families. He believes state
government should live within its means
and put taxpayers first.

The best decisions are often made
closest to the people they affect. Brian
supports returning more authority to local
communities, where families, small
businesses, schools, and local leaders
understand their needs better than
Olympia does. He believes state
government should empower
communities—not impose one-size-fits-
all mandates from afar.

Small businesses are the backbone of
local communities and should be able to
grow, hire, and serve customers without
being buried under unnecessary
regulations. Brian supports reducing
barriers that make it harder for local
employers to succeed. Stronger small
businesses mean stronger local
economies, more jobs, and healthier
communities.

Public safety starts with standing up for
victims, protecting the vulnerable, and
supporting those who keep our
communities safe. Brian believes
Washington needs a justice system that
puts victims first, holds criminals
accountable, and gives law enforcement
the tools they need to do their jobs
effectively. Families deserve to feel safe
in their homes, neighborhoods, and
communities.
