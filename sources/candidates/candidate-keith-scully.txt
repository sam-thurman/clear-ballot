Statement
I became a paramedic because I believed every life was
worth saving. That same conviction led me to prosecuting
war criminals, defending our environment, running for office.
In Shoreline, I expanded affordable housing and transit,
brought mental health professionals into the field, built
sidewalks and parks — all while balancing the budget.
Now I’ll take that record to Olympia. I’ll make housing,
energy, and childcare affordable. Fund education and
early learning. Demand real climate action. Reform our tax
code to be fair, progressive, and sustainable. Endorsed
by Snohomish Democrats, Shoreline and South County
Firefighters, Rep. Ryu and 40+ elected officials.
