Statement
I consider myself a pragmatic progressive Democrat. My
priorities include reducing corporate influence in politics,
regulating artificial intelligence to prevent unfair pricing, and
strengthening public services so every Washingtonian can
succeed.
Every Washingtonian deserves a great school, a walkable
neighborhood, an affordable home, and a government
that prioritizes people over profit. My experiences as a gay
man, the son of a teacher, and a resident director have
taught me the value of listening, serving others, and building
community. As your state representative, I will support bold
ideas and believe no problem is too big to solve.
