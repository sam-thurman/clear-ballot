Stacy is a banker, nonprofit president, daughter of a veteran, and proud resident of Washington’s 12th Legislative District. She has spent years building community and solving problems. Now she’s running to represent Washington’s 12th in the State House of Representatives.

MEET STACY

Government should work for everyday people, not special interests or party insiders. That’s what drives Stacy Willoughby.

​

A Washington native who grew up living all over the world , Stacy has called the mountains above Lake Chelan home for almost a decade, where she lives with her husband, Rick, their two dogs, and seven chickens.

​

With more than 25 years working in financial services, Stacy has seen firsthand how issues like housing affordability and rising taxes impact everyday people across our communities. She’s running to bring that real-world perspective to Olympia, and to make sure we aren’t an afterthought.

​

Stacy doesn't just talk about community — she’s built it. She brings years of hands‑on leadership in Chelan County and beyond. Through her volunteer work including serving as the Board President of Chelan Valley Hope, and her role on the Chelan Chamber of Commerce Board, Stacy has earned the trust of her neighbors across the district.

​

She loves working with families, small business owners, and community partners to tackle the challenges from Wenatchee and Chelan to Monroe and into King and Snohomish Counties. Her goal is to improve lives across rural and mountain communities and the growing suburban areas.

​

Stacy knows the pace of life is different in the 12th, and her leadership reflects our values: hard work, fiscal responsibility, and common sense. She’s dedicated to bringing our perspective to Olympia and doing what it takes to let our voices be heard.

​

Stacy’s priorities are straightforward:

Expanding housing options that make sense for each community.

Ensuring tax fairness for rural families and small businesses.

Cutting through excessive building codes and permitting burdens.

Providing excellent care for kids and older adults.

Creating more pathways into the trades so young people can earn a good living without taking on student loan debt.

​

One of the main principles of democracy is that everyone in the community has an opportunity to be heard. Stacy believes there is strength in our diversity and there’s space for all values and perspectives in the 12th District.

​

Progress at our pace, on our terms.

​

Tell Stacy what matters to you, and she'll take it to Olympia.

Creating Good Jobs and a Strong Local Economy

Small businesses, agriculture, and family-owned enterprises are the backbone of the 12th District, and Stacy is committed to helping them succeed. She supports smart, locally driven economic development that creates good jobs without sacrificing the outdoor heritage and quality of life that make this region special. Stacy will champion workforce training, small business support, and partnerships that give local employers and workers the tools they need to grow and stay rooted here.

A Home for Every Family

Housing should be stable, affordable, and available in every community. Stacy believes a safe place to call home is a foundation for working families, seniors, and first-time buyers. She will fight to increase housing supply, crack down on predatory lending practices, and invest in rural and workforce housing so no one has to choose between paying rent and putting food on the table.

Strengthening Schools and Real Opportunity

Every child deserves a quality education and a clear path to opportunity, no matter their ZIP code. Stacy supports fully funding public schools, backing teachers, and making sure classrooms have the resources students need to succeed. She also believes in strong vocational and career-connected education programs that prepare students for good-paying jobs and allow young people to build a future right here in the district.

Infrastructure That Keeps Us Connected

Reliable roads, safe bridges, and fast broadband aren’t extras — they’re essential. Stacy understands that infrastructure investments keep communities connected, support local businesses, and improve daily life. She will fight for smart, long-term investments in transportation and broadband that serve rural and growing communities alike and help every corner of the district stay connected to the modern economy.

Budgeting Responsibly

Stacy believes in using taxpayer dollars wisely and responsibly. She supports balanced budgets that invest in priorities like education, healthcare, and infrastructure while respecting the financial realities facing working families and seniors. Her approach is practical and transparent: fund what works, cut waste, and make sure public dollars deliver real value for working families.

FIGHTING FOR WHAT ACTUALLY MATTERS

Across the 12th District, families want good jobs, affordable healthcare, strong schools, safe communities, and a government that uses tax dollars responsibly. Stacy’s priorities are grounded in real life and shaped by listening to the people who live and work here.
