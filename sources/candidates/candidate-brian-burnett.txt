Statement
Brian Burnett proudly serves the 12th District, bringing
decades of public safety and leadership experience to
Olympia. A former law enforcement professional and current
Director of Law Enforcement Services, Brian works to
protect what matters most: your rights, your property, and
your role as a parent.
In the Legislature, he focuses on reducing crime, supporting
families, prioritizing taxpayer dollars, returning decision-
making to local communities, reducing burdens on small
businesses, and supporting victim-centered justice that
holds criminals accountable. Brian and his wife, April, have
been married 39 years and are proud parents, grandparents,
and lifelong Washingtonians.
