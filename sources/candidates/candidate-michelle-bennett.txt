Statement
Families in the 5th Legislative District deserve safer
communities, stronger schools, and a state government
that respects the people paying the bills. Olympia recently
voted for the largest tax increase in state history while many
families struggle with rising costs. Police have also been left
without clear tools to protect victims and neighborhoods.
With an education doctorate, and as a former police chief in
this district, I bring real-world experience in public safety,
education, and responsible budgeting. I will ensure state
spending delivers measurable results. I will fight for stronger
schools, safer communities, and will bring common-sense
leadership to Olympia.
