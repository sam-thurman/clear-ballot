Washington deserves Supreme Court Justices who will faithfully apply the law, protect constitutional rights, and treat every person who enters the courtroom with fairness and respect.
Todd Bloom has spent more than 25 years serving others as an attorney, CPA, small business owner, and Navy veteran. Throughout his career, he has helped families, small business owners, and individuals navigate complex legal issues while earning a reputation for integrity, thoughtful judgment, and professionalism.
Now, Todd is running for the Washington State Supreme Court to bring that same commitment to our state's highest court.
