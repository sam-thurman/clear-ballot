MEET NIRAV SHETH
Unity. Determination. Safety.
I firmly believe that our fight should not be against each other but for each other.
I was born and raised in India and came to the US in 2008 as a student of computer and electrical engineering where English became my fourth language. I joined the Marines and proudly served four years as an aircraft mechanic and military police officer. I then continued to serve the people as a police officer here in Lakewood, WA in 2017.
I found my home here in the United States and worked my way from being homeless into becoming a successful business owner in Pierce County. My passion has been to help others come out of similar poverty situations and improve the lives of all those around me. My goal running for office is to unite us by enabling each of us to achieve our dreams by creating safe communities, strong infrastructure, and a bright future for our next generation.

Issues That Matter To You
Public Safety
Supporting our emergency services and making crime illegal again in Washington State is crucial. By providing adequate funding and better equipment, we can ensure the safety of our communities and enable law enforcement and fire departments to respond effectively to emergencies.
Education
Collaborating with parents, educators, and local communities is key to creating an education system that prioritizes critical thinking skills and independent thought. Together, we can ensure a brighter future for our students.
Strong Infrastructure
Infrastructure is a critical foundation for a thriving economy. By investing in stronger and safer transportation systems, ensuring the resilience of our electric grid, and providing support for small businesses, we can create a conducive environment for economic growth.
Immigration
As an Immigrant, I know that legal immigration not only ensures a successful integration into our great nation but also upholds the values of fairness, opportunity, and diversity that make the U.S. a beacon of hope for those seeking a better life.
