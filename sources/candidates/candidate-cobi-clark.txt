Other Professional Experience
I’m a housing developer, construction project manager,
and South Sound Business Top 40 Under 40 honoree.
Throughout my career, I helped deliver hundreds of homes
across Washington State, including senior living, single-
family housing, market-rate apartments, and middle housing,
before pursuing a career in public service.
Education
I hold an interdisciplinary Bachelor’s degree in Economics,
Policy Analysis, Politics, and Philosophy from the University
of Washington Tacoma. During my studies, I worked as
policy staff for Senator Jeff Wilson and as a Business
Advocate for the Tacoma-Pierce Chamber of Commerce. My
academic work on public policy has been peer-reviewed and
published.
Community Service
Auburn Rotary
Statement
I’m running to restore affordability, protect constitutional
rights, and bring practical, policy-focused leadership
back to Olympia. After delivering hundreds of homes
across Washington, I’ve seen firsthand how excessive
regulations, rising taxes, and bureaucratic delays drive up
the cost of housing, energy, and healthcare. I support fiscal
responsibility, accountable government, strong civil liberties,
safe communities that respect both public safety and
individual rights, and continued opposition to a state income
tax. We need lower costs, abundant and reliable energy,
more housing, and less political theater. It’s time for a better
way forward that puts working families and individuals first.
