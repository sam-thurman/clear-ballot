Statement
I’ve led on major progressive wins. There’s more to do.
I championed the Millionaires and capital gains taxes to
make the wealthiest pay their fair share to fund schools,
healthcare, and childcare. I’m working to ensure our kids
can afford to live in Seattle and seniors can age in place by
building affordable housing, cutting sales tax, and making
college free. I’ve fought Trump by passing ICE mask bans,
funding abortion access, and protecting immigrants. I’ve
always stood up to special interests.
