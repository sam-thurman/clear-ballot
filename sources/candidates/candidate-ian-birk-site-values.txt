Values
Judicial Independence
Washington’s courts must remain fair, impartial, and independent. Judge Birk believes that judges must decide cases based on the law and the facts—never politics, pressure, or popularity. An independent judiciary is essential to protecting constitutional rights and maintaining public trust in the rule of law.
Equal Access to Justice
The justice system must work for everyone, not just those with resources or power. Throughout his career—as a lawyer, a longtime pro bono volunteer, and a judge—Judge Birk has worked to ensure that people from all walks of life have a meaningful opportunity to be heard in court. Equal access is foundational to equal justice.
Fairness and Respect for the Law
Fair outcomes require careful attention to the record, respect for legal precedent, and thoughtful reasoning. Judge Birk is committed to applying the law evenly and explaining judicial decisions clearly, so the public, lawyers, and lower courts can understand not just what the law is, but why it is applied that way.
Integrity and Accountability
Courts derive their legitimacy from the quality and integrity of their work. Judge Birk believes that judicial decision-making must be transparent, principled, and accountable to the law itself. Careful reasoning and well-written opinions are essential to maintaining confidence in the judiciary.
Understanding the Real-World Impact of Decisions
Judicial decisions affect families, workers, businesses, and communities across Washington. Judge Birk brings to the bench decades of experience seeing how the legal system operates in real people’s lives. That perspective informs his approach to judging with care, humility, and respect for the human consequences of the Court’s work.
Commitment to Excellence
Serving on Washington’s highest court requires rigor, preparation, and collaboration. Judge Birk believes that excellence in judging comes from deep engagement with the law, close collaboration with colleagues, and a lifelong commitment to learning and improvement.
