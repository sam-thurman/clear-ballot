Statement
Washington’s First District deserves leadership focused on
pragmatic solutions, not the emotionally triggering sideshow
antics that serve only to divide us. Our community thrives
when we move past partisan distractions and unite to tackle
the core economic and structural problems facing our nation.
For too long, short-sighted economic policies have allowed
multinational corporations to hollow out America’s middle
class from within. Offshoring has stripped away vital
manufacturing capabilities, leaving our local economies
vulnerable. I believe it is time to reverse this trend. By
implementing policy-driven solutions that incentivize
domestic production, we can return high-quality jobs to
Washington and rebuild our economic independence.
True economic resilience requires a foundation of security.
Our digital infrastructure is currently vulnerable to global
disruptions and foreign interference. I will prioritize robust
federal investments to safeguard our technology systems,
ensuring our grids, communications, and private data remain
secure and sovereign.
Furthermore, we must shift our economic focus away from
Wall Street financiers and billionaires who consolidate wealth
at the expense of Main Street. Offshore tax havens create
a two-tiered economic system which limits our strategic
capacity. My platform calls for addressing the tax problem
of the corporate welfare dependents who are costing our
country trillions in lost revenue. I will fight for small and
medium-sized businesses. These enterprises are the true
engines of our economy and the foundation of our local
diversity. By lowering regulatory barriers and expanding
access to capital for local entrepreneurs, we can foster a
diverse, self-sustaining marketplace rooted right here in
Washington State.
I am running to be a unifying voice in Congress. I am
committed to substantive policy that strengthens our
industries, secures our digital future, and elevates everyday
families over corporate interests. Together, we can reject
attempts to divide us and build a strong, prosperous future
for ourselves.
