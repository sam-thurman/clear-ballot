Statement
I was raised by a single mom who came to this country as
a refugee. As a child in Seattle, we experienced housing
insecurity, medical debt, and gun violence. We achieved
stability when she found a union healthcare job, and I joined
Rainier Scholars.
It’s time for change in Olympia, to address urgent needs and
restore hope: housing affordability for struggling families,
quality childcare for working parents, interest limits on
medical debt, and early interventions that keep kids learning.
Let’s do this, together. Endorsements: Representative Dawn
Mason, Chris Reykdal, Sage Leaders, College Democrats of
Washington, Jaelynn Scott, and Hannah Sabio-Howell.
