Statement
I’ve prioritized the health and safety of our families and
communities, passing laws that reduce gun violence,
improve police training and trust, and address root causes of
crime. Committed to addressing affordability, I’ve delivered
resources for housing and childcare, reduced small business
taxes, and expanded working family tax credits. Committed
to a strong economy, I work with local businesses, unions,
and schools to protect jobs, expand apprenticeships, and
provide career pathways.
Endorsed: 30th District Democrats, State Labor Council,
Planned Parenthood, OneAmerica Votes, Sierra Club,
housing advocates, teachers, healthcare workers, and more!
Let’s keep working together for the future we deserve.
