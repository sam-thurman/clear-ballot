CONTEST: State Representative | District 33 Position 1 | 2-year term
CANDIDATE: Edwin Obras (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
Edwin was appointed to the Washington State House in
December 2024 and elected in 2025.
Other Professional Experience
Edwin has spent last 18 years at the Seattle Human Services
Department, rising from program specialist to Division
Director.  He’s worked on homelessness, anti-hunger
programs, youth development, and community safety.
Education
BA, University of Washington; MPA, Northeastern University.
First in his family to graduate from college.
Community Service
Edwin came from the Philippines as a child and has lived
in SeaTac for the last 23 years. Past Board President of
Filipino Community of Seattle and co-chaired their affordable
housing capital campaign. Chaired the SeaTac Human
Services Advisory Committee.
Statement
Rent, housing, gas and groceries are skyrocketing.
Paychecks aren’t keeping up. Families can’t afford to
live in their own neighborhoods. In my first full session in
Olympia, six of my bills fighting for affordability and workers
became law. I protected isolated workers, strengthened
background checks, regulated rideshare companies, and
strengthened nursing title protections against AI. I’ll continue
fighting for immigrant rights. I secured over $30 million for
our District, including investments for housing and health.
I’ll keep showing up. Endorsed: WA State Labor Council,
33rd Democrats, Washington Conservation Action, Planned
Parenthood, Washington Nurses Association, Governor
Ferguson, Senator Orwall, Representative Gregerson.
