Elected Experience

Other Professional Experience
Lifelong blue-collar worker as an automotive technician,

my practical problem-solving skills that I will bring to the
Washington Legislature.
Education
Graduated high school 2010. Computer-Based Accounting

professional experience.
Community Service
Generous donor to conservative causes that deliver real
results locally and nationally. Through consistent tithing,
charitable giving, I frequently help those in need with cash,
vehicles, tires, clothing, and large gifts.
Statement
I am a blue-collar worker who turned my life around,
achieving homeownership through hard work and faith
in Jesus Christ. I know what it means to be forgotten by
Olympia. Our government ignores your God-given rights
and taxes away your hard-earned income. It is time to
replace career politicians with leaders who champion limited
government, traditional family values, fiscal responsibility,
public safety, and constitutional rights. I will fight to abolish
energy-killing taxes, support law enforcement, and protect
your family from government overreach. Endorsed by the
King County Republican Party. Together, we can restore
common sense and American values in Washington.
