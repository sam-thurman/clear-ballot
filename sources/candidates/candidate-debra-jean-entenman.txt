CANDIDATE: Debra Jean Entenman (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
State Representative, 47th District, 2018–present; member
of House committees on Housing; Civil Rights & Judiciary;
Transportation; and Postsecondary Education & Workforce.
Other Professional Experience
12 years of experience serving South King County as a
Congressional staffer, including three as the District Director.
Education
Bachelor’s Degree, Political Science, Seattle University.
Community Service
Current member, Washington State Board for Community &
Technical Colleges; 11 years on Renton Technical College
Board of Trustees; member, Urban League of Metropolitan
Seattle; member, National Association for the Advancement
of Colored People (NAACP).
Statement
As your State Representative, I have worked to strengthen
our social safety nets, improve public safety, support
workers, and invest in frontline responders. As a member of
the first Seattle/King County Head Start class and a non-
traditional college graduate, I know how life-changing early
access to opportunity and affordable education can be.
Through HB2015, I secured $100 million for public safety.
My bill HB1559 created benefits navigators to help college
students access wraparound support and complete their
education.
I will continue working with my colleagues across the aisle to
protect vulnerable households and improve opportunities for
families throughout Washington state.
