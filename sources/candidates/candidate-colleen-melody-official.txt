Justice Colleen M. Melody
Colleen Melody began serving as the 100th Justice of the Washington Supreme Court on January 1, 2026, following her appointment by Governor Bob Ferguson.
Prior to joining the Court, Justice Melody served for 11 years as Chief of the Civil Rights Division in the Washington State Attorney General’s Office. In that role, Justice Melody enforced federal and state laws protecting the rights of Washingtonians, with an emphasis on civil rights and anti-discrimination work. During Justice Melody’s tenure, the Civil Rights Division led some of the most visible and cutting-edge enforcement matters the office undertook. That included co-leading Washington’s multi-state lawsuit to protect the availability of abortion medication nationwide, as well as challenges to the federal government’s Muslim Travel Ban, Transgender Military Service Ban, effort to end birthright citizenship, and attempt to repeal the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals program (DACA).
Prior to joining the Attorney General’s Office, Justice Melody served as a trial attorney in the Civil Rights Division of the U.S. Department of Justice in Washington, DC. There, she brought civil lawsuits to remedy discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, religion, and disability.
Justice Melody has been recognized for her work to advance civil rights. Along with her colleagues, she received the Latina/o Bar Association’s Abogadas/os Excepcionales Award in 2022, the Legal Foundation of Washington’s Charles A. Goldmark Award in 2018, the American Immigration Lawyers Association’s Jack Wasserman Memorial Award in 2017, and the Council on American-Islamic Relations’ Defender of Liberty Award in 2017. Justice Melody received the Steward of Justice Award, the highest honor bestowed by the Attorney General’s Office, in 2018. She also received the Washington Law Review’s Distinguished Alumni Award in 2017 and the U.S. Department of Justice’s Special Commendation for Outstanding Service in the Civil Rights Division in 2012.
Justice Melody is a graduate of the University of Washington and the University of Washington School of Law, where she was an inaugural recipient of the Gates Public Service Law Scholarship for students dedicated to public-interest law. Following law school, she served as a law clerk to the Honorable Ronald M. Gould of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit.
Justice Melody was born and raised in Spokane, and now lives in rural Western Washington with her husband and two daughters.
