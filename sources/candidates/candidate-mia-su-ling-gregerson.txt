CONTEST: State Representative | District 33 Position 2 | 2-year term
CANDIDATE: Mia Su-Ling Gregerson (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
Mia Gregerson is a four-generation South King County
resident, adopted from Taiwan by an Air Force family. She
served on the SeaTac City Council, then as Mayor from 2008
to 2015, when SeaTac passed the first $15 minimum wage in
the country. She’s represented the 33rd District since 2013,
serves as Vice-Chair of House Appropriations, Co-Chairs a
statewide poverty reduction task force and helped establish
Washington’s first Office of Equity.
Other Professional Experience
Before the legislature, Mia spent nearly 20 years in the dental
field as a surgical assistant and business manager.
Education
She has a degree in history from the University of
Washington.

No information submitted
Statement
Times are tight. Groceries cost more. Gas costs more. Rent
costs more. Families in our district feel it every week at the
checkout line. The status quo isn’t working. I’ve worked
to deliver real results to help struggling families during
these challenging times. Free school meals for every kid
in Washington. Emergency food assistance when SNAP
benefits dropped. A Right to Repair law to save you money
on electronics. Free transit for kids. Millions for the 224th
corridor and Kent’s rapid ride bus. Endorsed: State Labor
Council, One America Votes, Adam Smith, Tina Orwall,
Edwin Obras, Bob Ferguson, and more.
