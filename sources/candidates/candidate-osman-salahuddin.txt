Statement
As your State Representative, I’m focused on delivering
results. I secured critical childcare and healthcare
investments for our district and passed more laws than any
legislator this year, championing bills on affordable housing,
gun violence prevention, and immigrant protections. As a
lifelong Eastsider, I’ll keep fighting for local priorities: public
safety, healthcare access, and strong schools. I’m running
to continue serving our communities with a commitment to
common-sense solutions and economic growth.
