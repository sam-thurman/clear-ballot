About Patrick
Meet Patrick
My family has lived continuously in Hobart, WA since 1887. Community service has always been part of our tradition, and thanks to my father, Dick Peacock, long-time president of the Maple Valley Historical Society, I've developed an in-depth understanding of the histories of the areas around Issaquah, Maple Valley, Ravensdale, Black Diamond, and Enumclaw.
I hold a Master of Business Administration with a finance emphasis from Webster University and a B.A. in Philosophy with an Epistemology focus from the University of Washington.
I served 23 years in the U.S. Army as a Human Intelligence Warrant Officer and Counter-Intelligence Agent, completing two combat tours in Afghanistan and earning the Bronze Star and Combat Action Badge. I currently serve as Vice Chair of the King County GOP and as a Senior Industrial Security Specialist at Boeing.

Priorities
What Patrick Will Fight For
Affordability First
No state income tax. Roll back fuel taxes that drive up the price of everything. Make Washington affordable for working families again.
Housing Within Reach
Cut the red tape blocking new construction and bring down the cost of putting a roof over your family's head.
Public Safety
Back our officers, hold repeat offenders accountable, and restore safe streets across the 5th District.
Quality Education
Focus our schools on reading, math, and real-world skills — and respect parents as partners in their kids' education.
Keep Employers Here
Stop driving employers out of Washington. Lower the cost of doing business so paychecks grow here at home.
Real Solutions
One-party rule has delivered the same failed results year after year. It's time for a change — let's do what works.
