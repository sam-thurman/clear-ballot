Statement
People across the 32nd are falling behind, wages aren’t
keeping up with bills, immigrants and the most vulnerable
in our community face federal targeting, and our current
leaders aren’t doing enough to help. We deserve better.
I’m a civil rights leader and one of the original plaintiffs
against Trump’s Muslim Ban. I’ve spent my career organizing
alongside labor, immigrant rights groups, and community
organizations fighting for the people our system too often
ignores. I’ll fight for housing affordability, lower everyday
costs, and a Washington that stands up to federal overreach.
Endorsed by Democrats, local leaders, and community
organizations.
