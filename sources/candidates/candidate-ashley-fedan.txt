Statement
For over two decades, I’ve served as a nurse helping local
neighbors and in the US Navy. I’ve worked with teams under
pressure while caring for my patients and families. We need
that experience fighting for working people squeezed by
housing and childcare costs, healthcare bills, and education
debt.
No nurses currently serve in the legislature. I’ll fight for
accessible healthcare, affordable housing, K-12 investments,
career training, and well-paying jobs. Nurses are trained to
solve problems- and are needed to bring positive change
to Olympia. Endorsed by Nurses, State Employees, Elected
Officials, 11th LD Dems, County Dems, Planned Parenthood,
more.
