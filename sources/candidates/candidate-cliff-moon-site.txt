About Cliff
A Neighbor. A Leader. A Voice for LD1.
Cliff Moon has served Legislative District 1 for 50 years and is raising his family in Lake Forest Park. He brings sixty-three years in the construction trades and fifty-four years in small business ownership to this race — along with degrees in Physical Oceanography and Civil Engineering from the University of Washington. He knows what it takes to build something, and he knows what government gets wrong when it loses sight of working families.
The cost of gas, groceries, and utilities is eating away at household budgets. Layers of taxes are driving up costs. Olympia's moral compass has been displaced, and common sense has been ignored. Cliff is running to change that.

The Issues
What Cliff Stands For
Four priorities driving this campaign — chosen because they matter most to the people of LD1.
Cost of Living & Tax Relief
The cost of gas, groceries, and utilities is eating away at family budgets. Cliff will fight to reduce the layers of taxes driving up costs for LD1 families.
Law Enforcement & Public Safety
Cliff fully supports law enforcement as the foundation of a safe community. He will stand with officers and restore accountability to the criminal justice system.
Family & Parental Rights
Families are the foundation of a strong community. Parents should raise their children, and schools should focus on academics — not agendas.
Small Business & Jobs
With 54 years in small business ownership, Cliff knows what it takes to create jobs. He'll cut red tape and create conditions for local businesses to thrive.
