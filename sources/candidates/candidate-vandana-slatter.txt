Statement
As your State Senator, I bring experience as a scientist,
healthcare professional, mom, and proud military parent to
address local priorities amid federal and economic uncertainty.
I have a strong record of fighting for our shared values:
affordable healthcare, reproductive freedom, support for
immigrants, and a tax code that is fair to working people.
I championed legislation to lower costs for working families,
strengthen public education and childcare, support innovation
and scientific research, ensure hospitals remain open, and
protect Washingtonians from harmful federal overreach. I voted
for more affordable housing, transit and transportation solutions
for our region, career training, strengthening gun safety laws,
climate action, and reducing medical debt. As lead sponsor of
“My Health My Data”--one of the nation’s strongest medical
privacy laws, and a constitutional amendment protecting
abortion rights, I’m a proven advocate for personal freedoms.
I’m a data-driven problem-solver representing our community’s
diversity and innovative spirit. Everyone deserves a chance to
thrive-I’ll keep fighting to make that possible.
