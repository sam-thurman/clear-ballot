Statement
Paying rent, buying a home, and raising a family should
be affordable. The reason they’re not is because of career
politicians who protect the status quo and cut deals with
corporate special interests that keep costs high. That’s why
we’re the only campaign in this race not taking corporate PAC
money.
In the Washington State Legislature, I worked to expand
healthcare access, invest in higher education, and tax
the ultra-rich. At Working Washington, I fought alongside
underpaid workers and took on big corporations to raise
wages, strengthen labor standards, and ensure all workers
have protections. I organized alongside grassroots workers’
rights, immigrants’ rights, and queer rights organizations to end
corporate tax breaks and defend Washington from Trump.
As your state senator, I’ll fight to ensure the ultra-wealthy pay
their fair share for good schools, healthcare, and childcare;
reduce rent and put homeownership within reach by building
more housing; and build toward universal childcare—ultimately
lowering costs and making Washington more affordable for all
of us.
We’re endorsed by progressive champions and unions like
State Representative Shaun Scott, Seattle City Councilmember
Alexis Mercedes Rinck, State Senator Rebecca Saldaña, Seattle
Port Commissioner Toshiko Hasegawa, AFT Washington, and
Working Families Party. More at hannahforwashington.com.
