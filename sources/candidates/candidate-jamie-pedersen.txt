CONTEST: State Senator | District 43 | 4-year term
CANDIDATE: Jamie Pedersen (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
Democratic Majority Leader (2025-present), State Senator
(2013-present), State Representative (2007-2013), 43rd District.
Other Professional Experience
Executive Vice President and General Counsel at McKinstry,
a Seattle-based engineering and construction firm whose
hundreds of skilled union workers excel in environmentally
responsible building (2012-present). Lawyer, K&L Gates (1995-
2012).
Education
Puyallup High School, Yale College, Yale Law School.
Community Service
Provided free legal assistance to local nonprofits, including
Lambert House and Seattle Men’s Chorus. Helped refugees
win political asylum. Lead volunteer attorney for Lambda Legal
in Washington state’s marriage equality case. Treasurer and
Sunday School teacher, Central Lutheran Church on Capitol Hill.
Scout troop leader. Bicycle commuter.
Statement
Jamie is widely recognized by labor unions as the most
progressive Senate Leader in state history. He led the effort to
pass the Millionaires Tax and a capital gains tax to make our
tax system fairer, provide funding for universal school lunches,
healthcare, and support for our most vulnerable communities
and small businesses, while cutting taxes for working families.
With Jamie’s leadership, our state protected Washingtonians
from Trump by banning ICE agents hiding behind masks,
stopping the use of cameras for surveillance and immigration
enforcement, restoring money Trump cut from Planned
Parenthood, and protecting sensitive voter information.
Jamie is a fierce champion for equality, leading the fight for
marriage equality, LGBTQ+ civil rights, and transgender privacy
protections. He’s delivered on school funding, gun violence
prevention, abortion rights, renter protections, climate action,
and Washington’s paid family leave program. But Jamie knows
the work isn’t finished. He is committed to amply funding
schools, tackling housing affordability, and building the full light
rail system approved by voters.
Jamie and his husband, a retired teacher, have four sons
in public schools, and live on Capitol Hill. Jamie has been
endorsed by labor unions, Sierra Club, Planned Parenthood
Alliance Advocates, and every Democratic State Senator.
