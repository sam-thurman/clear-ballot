Statement
Washington is becoming increasingly unaffordable, and
introducing a state income tax would only worsen the
situation. Residents already pay some of the highest fuel
taxes, which drives up costs across the board. To address
these challenges, we must encourage employers not to
leave and prioritize issues like affordable housing, quality
education, and reducing crime. Unfortunately, Washington’s
one-party rule has failed to deliver effectively on these
issues. Continuing to vote the same way and expecting
different results is futile—it’s time for a change. There are real
solutions, let’s do what works.
Endorsed by the 5th LD and King County Republicans.
