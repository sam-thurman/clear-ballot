Statement
I am honored to serve you in the State Senate, where I’ve
built on my record of legislative leadership– advancing
measures to address housing and behavioral health needs
and of veterans and military families and have worked to
create protections for our communities against unregulated
AI. I’m proud of this year’s bills that strengthen protections
against child sexual exploitation, prohibit insurance agencies
from using AI to deny care, invest in flood recovery,
reduce youth homelessness, and strengthen the rights of
homeowners.
As a career social worker, protecting vulnerable
Washingtonians has always been my focus. Protecting
survivors of sexual abuse, gun violence, and suicide is
absolutely a priority of mine whilst serving in the legislature.
With federal cuts threatening programs for struggling families
and agencies protecting vulnerable populations, I will
continue to work to strengthen and protect these resources
in our state.
With your vote, I’ll continue working on affordable housing,
comprehensive healthcare, 988, airport pollution mitigation,
childcare access for working families, language access in
schools, and finding climate solutions.
I’m proudly endorsed by local mayors and councilmembers,
unions and state leaders, and many more. Let’s keep
working together for stronger, safer, and more supportive
communities in South King County
