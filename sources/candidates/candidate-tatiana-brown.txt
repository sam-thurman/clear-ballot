Statement
I’m a community organizer who has spent my career fighting
alongside working families for justice, opportunity, and
turning grassroots advocacy into durable change.
In the 37th Legislative District, I’ve partnered with my
neighbors, workers, youth, and community leaders to help
shape groundbreaking policies like the Climate Commitment
and HEAL Acts. I’ve helped lead some of the nation’s first
government-funded community assemblies because the
people most impacted by public policy should help create it.
I’m running for State Senate to bring community voices
directly into Olympia and deliver on the priorities I hear
every day: affordable housing, stronger public schools,
safer neighborhoods, and reliable transit. I will champion
accessible healthcare, climate justice, and an economy
where working people can thrive. I will direct investments
into Seattle, Skyway and Renton, protect worker and
immigrant communities, small businesses, and ensure our
tax system is structured so the wealthiest pay their fair share.
The 37th is my home and I will work each day to fight for
a government that serves us all. You deserve leadership
centered in community and action, and with your vote – we
will Build Different together.
