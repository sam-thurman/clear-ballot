Statement
As your Representative, I’ve worked to deliver real results
for working families—expanding affordable housing,
strengthening worker protections, improving behavioral
health services, and defending civil rights. As a Navajo
attorney, I’ve spent my career standing up for justice and
speaking up for communities left out of decision-making.
I’ll continue fighting for strong schools, safer communities,
and an economy where every family has the opportunity to
succeed.
