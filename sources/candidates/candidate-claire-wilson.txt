Statement
Caring for our kids, families, and communities is what I do
every day– and with your support, I will continue fighting for
the people and places that matter. As a longtime educator,
former Federal Way School Board Director, and now your
State Senator, I place the needs of families and communities
first, focusing on solutions and results.
I lead Senate efforts to expand services and support for
young people, keeping kids in school and away from harm. I
sponsored and passed landmark investments in affordable
childcare, quality early learning, and help for vulnerable
families. I delivered resources for affordable housing options
that serve seniors, low-income families, working people, and
help people out of homelessness.
After devastating floods, I secured over $7.5 million in
funding for the city of Pacific. Committed to safety, I voted
for pay raises and additional training for law enforcement
officers, and funding for drug treatment and recovery. I
worked to reduce the tax burden on workers and small
businesses, while securing needed funding for K-12.
I’m proudly endorsed by Firefighters, Planned Parenthood,
Teachers, Nurses, Alliance for Gun Responsibility, Children’s
Campaign Fund, State Labor Council, Machinists. With your
vote, I’ll keep fighting– for all of us!
