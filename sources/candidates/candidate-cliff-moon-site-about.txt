About the Candidate
Meet Cliff Moon
Biography
A Lifetime of Building. Now Building for LD1.
Cliff Moon has served Legislative District 1 for 50 years. He has built his life here — raising his family in Lake Forest Park, running a small business, volunteering in the community, and watching his children grow up in the neighborhoods that make LD1 special.
Cliff's journey was never a straight line. While attending the University of Washington, he also worked as a journeyman steelworker, then worked his way through other trades. That experience taught him something no policy briefing can: what it actually costs to make ends meet, and what government gets wrong when it loses sight of the people it's supposed to serve.
Cliff holds a Bachelor of Science in Physical Oceanography and a Master of Science in Civil Engineering, both from the University of Washington, and brings sixty-three years in the construction trades and fifty-four years in small business ownership to this campaign. He has also served as a visiting faculty lecturer teaching Entrepreneurship and Introduction to Business at universities in Kazakhstan, India, and UW-Bothell.
"I am running to represent people that want to raise their kids, work hard, and have money left at the end of every paycheck."
Over the years, Cliff has seen his community change. Taxes and utility fees have soared, small businesses struggle under mounting regulations, and the paramount need for public safety has become more neglected for families across Lake Forest Park, Kenmore, Woodinville, and Bothell.
Cliff's campaign is built on four pillars: education reform with parental needs intimately considered, public safety, support for small businesses, and investment in infrastructure. These aren't abstract policy goals — they're community and neighborhood issues.

Track Record
A Career Built on Results
Cliff Moon has spent a lifetime building — in the trades, in business, in the classroom, and in his community.
63 Years in the Construction Trades
Cliff has spent over six decades working in the construction trades — understanding firsthand what it takes to build something from the ground up, manage costs, and deliver results on time and on budget.
54 Years in Small Business Ownership
As a small business owner for over five decades, Cliff has navigated payroll, regulation, taxation, and economic uncertainty — giving him a perspective on business that no career politician can match.
