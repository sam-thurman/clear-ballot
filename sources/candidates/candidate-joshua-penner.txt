Statement
I grew up in Bonney Lake, graduated from Sumner High
School, served as a Marine in Iraq, and led Orting as Mayor.
As your Representative, I bring our community’s shared
values and that fighting spirit to Olympia.
Together, we saved Buckley’s Rainier School, prevented
disastrous cuts for early education, protected care facilities
for fentanyl-exposed newborns, and cut red tape to fund
the repair of the White River and Fairfax bridges.  With your
support, I will keep fighting to lower living costs, demand
accountability, protect our most vulnerable, and secure our
neighborhoods. Let’s lead with action, not talking points.
