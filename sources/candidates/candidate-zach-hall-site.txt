REtain Zach Hall for State House
We’re so blessed to call Washington State home. With its incredible natural beauty, our access to the outdoors is unrivaled. We lead the nation in environmental stewardship and climate action. With a culture of innovation and thriving aerospace, agriculture, tech, and maritime industries, we consistently rank among the strongest and most diverse state economies. We’re committed to equality and opportunity.
We’re on the right track, but we’re not without our challenges. Families are still struggling, still stretching every dollar they earn. Our housing crisis is still as urgent as ever, access to affordable childcare and healthcare is still out of reach for too many, and our schools still need more funding to engage, support, and prepare our students for the future. I’m running to retain my seat to continue fighting for safer, more affordable communities.
ON THE ISSUES
PROVEN TRACK RECORD
Zach was appointed to the Washington State House of Representatives in June 2025. In his first session, he sponsored and passed bipartisan bills to lower energy costs, prioritized safety and congestion improvements along State Route 18, and supported reforms to help fix our housing crisis, modernize our power grid, and build light rail faster.
Before joining the state legislature, he served for six years on the Issaquah City Council, leading efforts to expand funding for affordable housing, public safety, environmental protection, and transportation infrastructure. He also chaired the Eastside Fire & Rescue Board of Directors, ensuring our first responders were equipped with the tools and resources they needed to keep families safe.
