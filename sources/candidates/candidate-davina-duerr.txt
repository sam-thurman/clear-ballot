CONTEST: State Representative | District 1 Position 1 | 2-year term
CANDIDATE: Davina Duerr (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
Appointed to represent the 1st District in 2019. Chair of
Local Government Committee. Served on Bothell City
Council 2016-2022, Deputy Mayor from 2016-2020.
Other Professional Experience
Only licensed architect in the legislature. Committed
to sustainable, affordable design. Served on Eastside
Transportation Partnership; WSDOT Executive Advisory
Group; Sound Transit Bus Rapid Transit Elected Leadership
Group; PSRC Transportation Policy Board and Board of
Directors of the Sound Cities Association
Education
Syracuse University, Bachelor of Architecture with a minor in
Environmental Geography
Community Service
Ten years on the Bothell Landmarks Preservation Board,
eight as chair. Co-founded the Northshore Schools
Foundation M.I.L.K. Money campaign supporting students
experiencing homelessness.
Statement
Quality of life depends on having affordable homes near
jobs, accessible transportation, a healthy environment,
and strong public education. Last term, seven of my bills
became law, including bills that: make it cheaper to build
affordable housing, phase out super-pollutant refrigerants,
close a growth management loophole, streamline permits
for new housing, and give Ecology enforcement over landfill
regulation. Secured $532 million for the 1st District in 2025,
including $464.5 million for the I-405 corridor project. Would
be honored to have your vote. Endorsed: State Labor
Council, Washington Conservation Action, Sen. Stanford,
Rep. Kloba, Mayors Thompson, Arndt, Herbig, Curtis and
others.
