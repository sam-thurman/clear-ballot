Statement
It has been a privilege to represent the people of the 36th
District for the past 10 years. I’ve been honored 15 times for
my advocacy for individuals with developmental disabilities
and behavioral health challenges, and youth involved in the
foster care and juvenile criminal legal systems, as well as
for my work on affordable housing, economic development
and rebalancing the state’s regressive tax code. I would be
honored to earn your vote.
The affordability crisis is hitting our district hard and is
being exacerbated by the reckless actions of the Trump
Administration. As a tax policy leader, I helped pass the
Millionaires Tax to fund our public schools, healthcare,
human services and early learning, as well as key
affordability policies like expanding the working families tax
credit (460,000 new households) and senior property tax
exemption program (50,000 new households), establishing a
low-income energy assistance program, historic investments
in affordable housing, and exempting key household items
from sales tax. If given the opportunity to continue to serve,
I’ll fight every day for the working people of our community.
I am proud to be endorsed by 36th District Democrats,
Working Families Party, Washington State Labor Council
AFL-CIO, and Washington Conservation Action.
