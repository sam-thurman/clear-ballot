Statement
As a mom with experience in government, nonprofits, and
tech, I have the skills and perspective to champion our
community’s priorities. On the Redmond City Council, I’ve
delivered affordable housing, expanded bike paths and
transit, increased public safety, and accelerated climate
action. As your Representative, I’ll work to support schools,
advance childcare and household affordability, protect
forests and farmland, and defend civil rights.
Endorsed: 45th Democrats, Congresswoman DelBene,

Education Association, State Labor Council, and more!
