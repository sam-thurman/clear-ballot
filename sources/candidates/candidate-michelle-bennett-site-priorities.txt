Protecting Families from an Expanding Income Tax Stop the Expansion of Washington’s Income Tax Oppose expansion of Washington’s income tax. Protect working families and small businesses from new tax burdens. Demand accountability before asking taxpayers for more. Prioritize spending on core services instead of expanding government beyond what families can afford. Fiscal Responsibility and Accountable Government Families make hard choices every month, and the state government should be expected to do the same. Michelle will push for a more disciplined, transparent approach to budgeting. Fund core priorities first before expanding government programs. Require accountability for state spending so taxpayers know where their money goes. Measure results to ensure programs are actually working. Eliminate waste and duplication across state government. Make government easier to navigate for families, small businesses, and local communities. Public Safety and Safe Communities Every person deserves to feel safe in their neighborhoods, schools, parks, and place of work. Michelle believes public safety is a basic responsibility of government — not a partisan issue. Support law enforcement, firefighters, and emergency responders who keep communities safe. Address property crime and public disorder with practical, accountable solutions. Improve accountability in the justice system so laws are enforced fairly and consistently. Support prevention and treatment where appropriate without excusing crime or reducing public safety. Strong Schools and Parents as Partners Michelle believes schools should prepare students for success in life, work, college, trades, and citizenship — with high standards, safe classrooms, strong teachers, and parents treated as essential partners. Focus on student achievement and learning recovery so every child has the opportunity to succeed. Support teachers and classroom discipline to create safe, productive learning environments. Expand pathways into trades, apprenticeships, and career-connected learning for students pursuing different futures. Respect parents’ role in education and keep families involved in decisions that affect their children. Affordability, Housing, and Quality of Life Families should not have to leave the communities they love because state policy keeps driving up the cost of living. Michelle will support practical solutions that protect local communities and help families, seniors, and young people build a future here. Reduce unnecessary regulatory costs that make housing, services, and daily life more expensive. Support practical housing solutions that fit local communities. Protect seniors and working families from being priced out of the places they call home. Support infrastructure planning that keeps pace with growth, like the Public Trust Fund. Local Businesses and Local Jobs Businesses, large and small, are the backbone of local communities. Michelle will support policies that make it easier to start, grow, and sustain a business in Washington — not harder. Reduce unnecessary red tape that slows down local employers. Oppose new tax burdens on local businesses and job creators. Support workforce development and career pathways that connect people with good jobs. Promote policies that help local businesses stay open and grow.
