Statement
I’m Maggie Wang – immigrant, business owner, wife,
mother and proud American. After arriving from Taiwan in
1989, my husband and I achieved the American Dream
through hard work. Today, that dream is under siege by
rising taxes, housing costs, and government overreach. We
need leadership prioritizing your budget over bureaucratic
expansion.
In Olympia, I will be your voice for affordability, public
safety, parental rights. My focus is simple: lower taxes,
safer neighborhoods, and an economy where small
businesses thrive. Let’s restore common-sense government
together. Endorsed by the King County Republican Party. I
am ready to fight for your family.
