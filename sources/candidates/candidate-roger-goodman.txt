Statement
I’m honored to represent you in Olympia, leading efforts to
improve public safety, reduce gun violence and help people
in crisis. I’ve passed laws to protect survivors of crime,
expand drug and mental health services, and improve law
enforcement accountability and training.
I voted to reduce the tax burden on working people and
small businesses and to provide free school meals and
more affordable childcare. With your vote, I’ll continue to
champion K-12 funding, affordable housing investments,
climate action, and reproductive rights. Let’s keep working
together to protect our communities and families.
