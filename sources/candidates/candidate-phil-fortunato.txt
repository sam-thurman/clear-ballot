Statement
It’s been an honor to serve as your voice in the State Senate
and stand up for the values, freedoms, and common
sense that make our communities strong.  My approach to
decision-making is simple: God first, the district second, and
personal preference last. That principle guides every vote
I take in Olympia. It’s why I regularly seek your feedback
through district surveys and town halls.  Your voice should
shape the decisions that affect our communities.
The financial challenges facing our state are not accidental.
They stem from excessive spending and the unchecked
growth of government programs. Individuals, families,
seniors, workers, and small businesses are all being crushed
by rising taxes and higher costs and for many of us, we’ve
reached the breaking point.
I will continue to stand firmly against efforts to erode our
Second Amendment rights, and I will fight for responsible,
disciplined use of your tax dollars. I ask for your continued
support as I work to protect your rights, defend your
prosperity, and fight for a more accountable state
government.
Proudly endorsed by law enforcement leaders, local elected
officials, community leaders, and neighbors across the
district who trust me to represent their values at the state
Capitol.
