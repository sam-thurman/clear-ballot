Statement
The 45th District deserves results, not partisan games. I am
not a politician; I am a teacher, mother, and grandmother
who believes in putting people first. I will restore
accountability to Olympia by defending our constitutional
rights, transparency, and equal justice. We must implement
secure elections, empower parents, and prioritize academic
excellence. I will fight the regulations driving up housing
costs and champion affordable energy. We need proven
recovery solutions for homelessness and full support for our
law enforcement. Endorsed by the King County Republican
Party. I am ready for the hard work of restoring trust and
defending our freedom.
