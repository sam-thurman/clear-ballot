Statement
As State Representative, I remain committed to safe,
affordable, thriving communities. With your support, I’ll
build on my expertise in supporting youth and workforce
development, helping the next generation reach their
full potential. I’ll advance pro-housing policies to meet
Seattle’s urgent and long-term needs, invest in pathways
to apprenticeship and higher ed, expand Washington’s
creative economy, and be a strong voice for climate-friendly
transportation solutions and the maritime economy.

LD Democrats,  legislators, labor & community leaders,
more!
