Statement
Washington families deserve to live in a state where hard
work can still afford a home, fill a gas tank, and build a better
future for their children.
For too long, Olympia has made life more expensive while
families fall further behind. Michael Rutland will fight to lower
costs, oppose new income taxes, repeal policies driving up
fuel and grocery prices, and demand responsible budgeting
instead of higher taxes on working people. Washington
taxpayers deserve relief, accountability, and leaders who
understand the pressure families and small businesses face
every day.
Every child deserves a great education, and every parent
deserves a voice. Michael will support high academic
standards, safe schools, career and technical education, and
parental involvement so students are prepared for college,
careers, and life. He believes schools should focus on
helping every student succeed, not political agendas coming
from Olympia.
Safe neighborhoods, affordable living, and accountable
government should not be partisan issues. This election
is a chance to bring balance, common sense, and a new
direction for Washington, so Washington works for working
people again.
