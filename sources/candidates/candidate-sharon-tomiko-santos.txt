Statement
In these tough times, Washington families face rising costs
and complex challenges. As your State Representative and
the House Education Chair, I am focused on a future where
economic security and quality education are accessible
to everyone. I am working to lower the cost of living while
protecting the essential services upon which we all rely.
Our path forward requires equity expertise and bold,
practical solutions. My deep community roots shape my
commitment to modernizing our workforce, increasing
our housing supply, and ensuring tax fairness for working
families. I respectfully ask for your vote to build a resilient,
inclusive Washington.
