Where Cliff Stands
A Clear Agenda for LD1
These priorities were shaped by conversations with residents across Lake Forest Park, Kenmore, Woodinville, and Bothell — the people who know this district best.
Cost of Living & Tax Relief
The cost of gas, groceries, and utility fees is eating away at family incomes. Layers of taxes are driving up costs for homeowners, renters, and small businesses alike. Cliff will fight to reduce the tax burden on working families and push back against policies that make everyday life more expensive.
Law Enforcement & Public Safety
Cliff fully supports law enforcement and public safety as the foundation of an active and safe community. He will stand with officers, restore accountability in the courts, and oppose policies that undermine the ability of local law enforcement to protect LD1 families.
Family & Parental Rights
Families are the foundation of a strong community. Cliff believes parents should raise their children — not government. He will defend parental rights in education and oppose state overreach into family life.
Education Focused on Academics
Schools should focus on academics — period. Cliff supports a curriculum centered on reading, math, science, and civics, and believes teachers and parents — not Olympia bureaucrats — should have the greatest say in what happens in the classroom.
Small Business & Economic Growth
With fifty-four years in small business ownership, Cliff knows what it takes to make payroll, navigate regulation, and create jobs. He will cut red tape, reduce the B&O tax burden on small operators, and create conditions where local entrepreneurs can hire, grow, and thrive.
Legal Immigration
Cliff supports legal immigration as a cornerstone of a safe and vibrant community. He believes in an orderly, lawful immigration system that welcomes people through the right channels while protecting the rule of law and the security of LD1 residents.
Infrastructure & Transportation
With an MSci in Civil Engineering and decades in the construction trades, Cliff understands infrastructure from the ground up. He will prioritize smart investment in LD1's roads, bridges, and transit connections — projects that move people efficiently and safely.
Common Sense in Olympia
Olympia's moral compass has been displaced and common sense has been ignored for too long. Cliff will bring the straightforward, results-oriented approach of a business owner and engineer to state government — cutting waste, demanding accountability, and putting LD1 families first.
