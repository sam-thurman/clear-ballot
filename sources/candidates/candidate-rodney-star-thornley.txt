Statement
I’m running as an independent because Olympia needs
a culture change. Too many elected officials fall in
line with party leadership instead of focusing on
the people they serve. I know this will be a steep
climb as a first-time candidate, but I’m ready to
do the work. My priorities are Washington State
citizenship for undocumented residents, a three-year
college path, fiscal accountability, and a government
that listens to citizens before it spends their money.
Voters deserve practical solutions, honest debate, and
representatives who aren’t controlled by party politics.
Visit my website for more details about my positions.
