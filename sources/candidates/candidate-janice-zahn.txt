Statement
I’m honored to represent you in Olympia, building a
Washington where every family can thrive and strengthening
our democracy. As an engineer, transportation leader,
and longtime public servant, I am focused on improving
affordability and delivering investments in housing,
education, childcare, and transportation, while addressing
our state’s budget challenges responsibly. As a first-
generation immigrant, I believe in people-first solutions
bringing communities together and getting things done.
