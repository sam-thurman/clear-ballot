Statement
It’s a privilege to represent you in the State Senate. As a
lifelong advocate for affordable housing and economic
opportunity, I bring deep experience and a proven track
record of delivering results. As your Senator, I’ve moved
us forward on what matters: high-quality public schools,
affordable housing, and access to childcare and healthcare.
I’m proud to have championed policies that address the
high cost of living by passing historic tax reform to fund
schools and services, tax relief for working families and
small businesses, and protections for renters, workers, and
consumers.
I will continue to push for a fair economy with good-paying
jobs and reforms that make homeownership, healthcare,
childcare, and groceries more affordable. I’ll fight to improve
educational outcomes for kids and strengthen the social
safety net for youth and seniors. We all deserve reliable
public services, and I’ll keep advocating for the investments
needed to make our communities healthy and safe.
You can count on me to stand up for our democracy and civil
rights, for reproductive freedom, common sense gun reform,
and climate action. I’m committed to making government
work for people—not special interests. I’d be honored to
earn your continued support.
