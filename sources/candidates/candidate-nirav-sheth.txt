Statement
My goal is to create sustainable & affordable housing to
solve homelessness, bringing law & order back to Seattle &
cheaper cost of living for everyone.
My name is Nirav Sheth. My journey from a modest home
in India to this stage was built on one belief: the American
Dream is not a gift—it is a promise worth fighting for. I
didn’t just come to this country; I earned my place here. I
swore an oath to protect our nation, first as a United States
Marine and later as a Police Officer. I know the grit it takes
to keep a community safe and the discipline required to lead
when there is no room for error. Today, our district is at a
breaking point. If you believe our community is thriving, stay
the course. But if you feel the higher cost of living & energy,
unsafe neighbourhood, and the fading light of opportunity, it
is time to turn the tide. I’ve felt this failure personally. My
business, Bistro Baffi, was a victim of the very policies that
are driving employers away and letting our neighborhoods
crumble. I am also running for Congress to bring military-
grade accountability to Washington, D.C. I also want to
work on Real Solutions to help homeless people & Energy
Independence.
In the Marines and in business, I’ve managed budgets where
every cent mattered. I will treat your tax dollars with that
same sacred respect. My life has been defined by service;
now, I ask for the honor of being your voice.
Together, we will secure a legacy of prosperity for our
children and bring growth back to our community.
