LEGISLATIVE PRIORITIES

Education

Building stronger schools and developing a skilled workforce for the future of North Central Washington.

Small Business

Reducing red tape and lowering tax burdens so local businesses can grow and create jobs.

Balanced Budgets

Supporting responsible budgeting and practical fiscal policies that protect taxpayers.

Healthcare

Expanding affordable healthcare options while improving transparency, choice, and cost containment.

Infrastructure

Investing in roads, transportation, and infrastructure critical to our communities and economic future.

Mike’s legislative priorities focus on strengthening North Central Washington through practical, forward-thinking policies that support families, workers, students, and small businesses.

His work in Olympia centers on improving education and workforce development, supporting responsible budgeting, reducing unnecessary regulatory burdens on employers, expanding economic opportunity, and investing in transportation infrastructure critical to the long-term success of communities throughout the 12th District.

Mike has spent his career advocating for small businesses and understands firsthand the important role they play in creating jobs, supporting local families, and strengthening communities. He believes state government should be a partner in economic success, not a barrier to it. That means creating a regulatory and tax environment that allows businesses to grow, hire employees, and remain competitive in Washington State.

Mike is also committed to improving access to affordable healthcare while protecting choice and working toward long-term cost containment for both employers and consumers.

As a lifelong resident of the district, Mike is proud to represent the communities that shaped him. He remains committed to listening, collaborating, and delivering meaningful results for the people of the 12th Legislative District.

Education and Workforce Training

As a member of the Education Committee, Mike worked to address and help satisfy the McCleary court decision requiring ample funding for education in Washington State. While important progress has been made, there is still more work to do.

Mike believes education funding should create additional opportunities for students while supporting clear, rigorous academic standards that prepare young people for long-term success.

Students and teachers in the 12th District deserve the resources they need to succeed, but it is equally important to ensure every dollar invested delivers meaningful results and reflects the changing landscape of education. Mike supports continued expansion of STEM programs and applauds the many schools throughout the district already leading the way in these areas.

Mike supports creating additional opportunities for youth employment and hands-on training programs that encourage innovative, work-based learning strategies. A strong local economy depends on developing and maintaining a skilled workforce.

He also believes increases to the minimum wage must be approached thoughtfully and collaboratively, with consideration for the impact on small businesses, job creation, cost of living, and long-term economic sustainability throughout Washington State.

Building Strong Communities

Through Sustainable Economic Growth

Small Business

Employers throughout the 12th District are creating jobs, expanding opportunities, and strengthening the local economy. A vibrant private sector is essential to building a sustainable and prosperous future for communities across North Central Washington. Small businesses continue to play a critical role in supporting local families, communities, and the state economy.

Mike understands the challenges facing small businesses throughout the district, including healthcare costs, overregulation, taxes, workforce shortages, education, and access to skilled employees.

As a representative in Olympia, Mike advocates for policies that support economic growth, encourage innovation, reduce unnecessary barriers for employers, and create a business environment where small businesses can succeed, create jobs, and continue giving back to the communities they serve.

Transportation

Freight mobility throughout the 12th District is essential to the success of the agricultural industry, local businesses, and the overall regional economy. Mike has worked to secure funding for transportation and infrastructure projects that directly impact the daily lives of residents and support long-term economic growth across North Central Washington.

Mike continues to work closely with local leaders, transportation councils, and state agencies to better understand and prioritize the transportation needs of the district. Reducing congestion, improving infrastructure, and increasing efficiency remain important priorities for communities throughout the region.

He also supports reforms that help reduce project costs and unnecessary delays while ensuring transportation investments are delivered responsibly and effectively. Mike remains focused on protecting affordability for families, farmers, and businesses by opposing policies that would significantly increase transportation and energy costs throughout the district.

Healthcare

Healthcare remains one of the fastest-rising costs facing both small and large businesses across Washington State. Many employers continue to struggle with the growing expense and complexity of providing quality healthcare coverage for their employees, while also navigating ongoing changes to federal and state healthcare regulations.

Mike recognizes the critical role healthcare providers and local healthcare systems play throughout the 12th District, not only in caring for residents, but also as major employers and economic drivers within many communities.

Mike continues to work alongside healthcare systems, public hospital districts, providers, and community leaders to ensure they have the resources and support needed to serve patients effectively and remain sustainable for the future. He also supports efforts to improve Washington’s healthcare system by increasing affordability, protecting access to care, and creating practical solutions that benefit both providers and patients.

Tourism & Hospitality

Tourism and hospitality are major economic drivers throughout the 12th District, supporting local businesses, creating jobs, and strengthening communities across North Central Washington. Mike worked alongside fellow legislators to help secure funding for a new statewide tourism initiative aimed at promoting Washington communities and supporting local economies.

In Chelan County alone, tourism generates hundreds of millions of dollars in economic activity each year and supports thousands of jobs throughout the region. From hotels and restaurants to wineries, outdoor recreation, and small businesses, tourism plays a vital role in the long-term economic success of the district.

Mike believes tourism should continue to be a legislative priority, as it remains one of Washington State’s largest industries, generating billions in economic impact and supporting more than 170,000 jobs statewide. Investing in tourism promotion and infrastructure helps create opportunity for local businesses, strengthen rural economies, and showcase the unique communities that make Washington special.
