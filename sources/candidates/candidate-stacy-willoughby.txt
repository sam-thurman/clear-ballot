CANDIDATE: Stacy Willoughby (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
First-time candidate
Other Professional Experience
Twenty-five years in financial services, including roles as
a financial advisor and local bank branch manager. She
understands how housing affordability, insurance costs, and
rising taxes impact families and communities.
Education
Graduate of the Leadership Institute of North Central
Washington. Attended Central Washington University and
Skagit Valley College.
Community Service
Board President of Chelan Valley Hope, a nonprofit
that supports families with essentials such as rent
assistance, fuel, firewood, food, and eviction-prevention
services, Founder of Chelan Business Women Connect,
and Board member of the Lake Chelan Chamber of
Commerce.
Statement
Politicians in Olympia are failing families here at home. They
promise “affordability,” yet gas and grocery prices soar,
housing remains out of reach, and good-paying jobs for
young people are vanishing. After years in financial services,
Stacy knows the numbers and her neighbors. She will
champion a state budget that prioritizes tax fairness for rural
families and small businesses, ensures affordable housing
that serves each community, and promotes trades training
for our youth.
Endorsements: 12th Legislative District Democrats, King
County Democrats, Washington State Labor Council, AFT-
Washington, and Sage Leaders Washington.
