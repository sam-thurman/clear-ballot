Statement
I’m in Congress to fight for you and to put a check on this
administration’s unchecked power. Right now, families are
struggling to get by, let alone get ahead. That’s why I’ve
introduced legislation to immediately suspend the gas tax
through the end of the year, spur clean energy development,
crack down on insurance company abuses, and create more
affordable housing.
Your tax dollars should work for you. That’s why I secured
over $60 million for Community Projects across the 8th
District: Graham’s school based health clinic, Ellensburg’s
Veterans’ housing, Highway 2 improvements, multiple food
bank expansions, North Central Washington firefighting and
wildfire resiliency, and body cameras and a King County
rapid DNA system to support law enforcement.
I’m standing up for family farms, which are foundational to
the 8th District, by reversing India’s apple tariffs, fighting the
Albertson’s-Kroger merger, and getting support for foreign
market access in the Farm Bill.
Healthcare costs are too high, so I’ve passed bills to
cap insulin costs, give Medicare the power to negotiate
lower prescription prices, and cap out of pocket costs
for seniors. But this Congress, my Republican colleagues
slashed your healthcare to give a tax break to billionaires.
That’s outrageous. I will always fight to lower costs and put
doctors and patients back in the driver’s seat, not insurance
companies.
With over 130 town halls, I’m committed to listening,
learning, and delivering for you in Congress. It is an honor to
serve you, and I humbly ask for your vote.
