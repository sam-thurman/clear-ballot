CANDIDATE: Suzan DelBene (Prefers Democratic Party)

Elected Experience
United States Representative, 2012-Present. Ways and
Means Committee.
Other Professional Experience
Successful 20-year career as a businesswoman and
entrepreneur. Former Microsoft executive, led local high-tech
startups. Former Director of Washington’s Department of
Revenue, where I led efforts to simplify the tax system and
help small businesses. Advisor to a microfinance non-profit
helping struggling families.
Education
B.A., Biology, Reed College; M.B.A., University of
Washington.
Community Service
I’ve mentored students at UW Business School; been active
in my church, serving as a board member. Volunteered with
the PTA, Girl Scouts and YWCA, supporting transitional
housing, job training and services to help families get back
on their feet.
Statement
Our country is at a crossroads. Congressional Republicans
refuse to stand up to Trump as he causes deep harm to our
economy, our families, our neighbors, and our democracy.
Because of them, costs are skyrocketing on gas, groceries,
housing, and utility bills. To pay for massive tax cuts for
the wealthiest few, Republicans ripped away health care
from millions, passed cruel cuts to take food off the table of
working families, and took away critical investments in green
energy.
As your Congresswoman, I’m fighting back against the
extreme policies that are making life unaffordable and
needlessly dividing Americans. I’m focused on lowering
costs, helping middle-class families and seniors, supporting
veterans, and building a bright future for our children.
I support policies to lower the cost of living, reduce taxes for
working families and ensure the wealthy pay their fair share.
I’m fighting to lower health care premiums, roll back the cruel
cuts to Medicaid, and end President Trump’s reckless tariffs.
To help working families, I’ve led on permanently expanding
the Child Tax Credit, which would send monthly checks
to families and cut child poverty in half. I’ve introduced
bipartisan legislation to spur the creation of two million
affordable homes. It’s past time to reform our immigration
system and rein in out-of-control ICE agents who have
spread fear and violence in our communities. Unlike MAGA
Republicans, I’m committed to strengthening Social Security
and Medicare. I strongly support reproductive rights and
will do everything I can to fight back against Republican
extremists’ attempts to pass a nationwide abortion ban.
I am honored to have endorsements from Democratic
groups, labor unions, local leaders, and many others.
With a focus on results, not rhetoric, I’m confident we can
make real progress to help everyone in the 1st District. I ask
for your vote.
