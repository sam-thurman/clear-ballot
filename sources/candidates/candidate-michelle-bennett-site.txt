The 5th District First

My job isn't to represent a political party. My job is to represent you.

Michelle Bennett is running for State Representative in Washington’s 5th Legislative District to protect working families, defend taxpayers, elevate public safety and bring practical, accountable leadership to Olympia.

Families across the 5th Legislative District are working harder than ever, but Olympia keeps making life more expensive. From higher taxes to rising energy, housing, and everyday costs, too many decisions in state government are being made without enough concern for the people who have to pay the bill. Michelle Bennett believes Washington needs a different approach: protect taxpayers, fund core priorities, support public safety, strengthen schools, and make government more accountable to the people it serves. And Michelle serves everyone in the 5th Legislative District, not a party affiliation. She is running as a Republican to reflect her fiscal values and the needs of the people in the 5th.

Michelle will oppose efforts to expand Washington’s income tax and fight for tax policies that protect working families, seniors, small businesses, and local communities.

State government should set priorities, measure results, and respect taxpayers. Michelle will push for disciplined budgeting and practical solutions.

Michelle supports law enforcement, first responders, local schools, small businesses, and the infrastructure families rely on every day.

A Different Direction for Washington

The current direction in Olympia is clear: more taxes, more spending, and less accountability. Michelle Bennett believes families in the 5th District deserve a representative who will ask harder questions, protect household budgets, and stand up when state government goes too far.
