Statement
I am honored to represent Eastside families and committed
to building the safe, supportive communities where everyone
can thrive. As a healthcare professional, protecting access
to affordable and high-quality care, supporting scientific
innovations, and taking meaningful action on climate change
are my top priorities. As the first refugee elected, I am
committed to advancing a more just, inclusive, and equitable
society for all.
With your support, I will prioritize the well-being of our
community - focusing on educational opportunities,
expanding behavioral health services, improving
transportation, and developing strong workforce across our
region. I humbly ask for your vote.
