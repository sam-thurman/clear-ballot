Statement
It’s an honor to represent you and fight for our communities.
My record is one of an effective progressive who delivers
results. I passed the nation’s strongest climate laws;
defended Washington from federal attacks; secured
investments in education, healthcare, transit, public safety,
and the environment; and helped stabilize our budget.
I led passage of landmark tax reform — a millionaires’
tax paired with tax cuts for working families and small
businesses. A new parent of two young children, I’m fighting
for safe communities, better school funding, affordable clean
energy, fair taxes, and a sustainable future. I ask for your
vote.
