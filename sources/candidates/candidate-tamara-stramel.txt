Statement
I’m running for State Senate in the 31st district because I
believe our communities deserve a senator who shows up,
listens, and works for everyone who calls this district home.
Families are being priced out, infrastructure is straining under
rapid growth, and our schools are asked to do more with
less. At the same time, too many people feel unheard, or
afraid to speak up.
I’m a mom, an organizer, and a veteran spouse. I’ve worked
as a youth counselor with kids in crisis, supported military
families through deployments, and spent eight years putting
down roots near Lake Tapps, raising my two boys with
my husband. Our current challenges aren’t abstract policy
debates to me; they are my life.
I believe we can govern better. I will lead with honesty,
compassion, and determination to solve problems while
representing the whole 31st district. I will advocate for
our communities to be prioritized by Olympia, particularly
in the transportation budget. We deserve leadership that
cares about families, champions education, and takes rural
infrastructure development seriously. I would be honored to
earn your vote.
Endorsed: Washington State Labor Council, AFL-CIO; Pierce
County Democrats; and trusted local leaders. Please see my
website for more!
