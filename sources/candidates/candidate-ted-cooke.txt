Statement
Democrats made Washington unaffordable. I have a plan
to fix it. I’ll freeze all new taxes and eliminate existing taxes
on income, property, and fuel. I’ll order audits of every state
office and program and cut waste. I’ll end unaccountable
public-private partnerships and legislative commissions.
I’ll prioritize starter homes with yards over high-density
apartments forced on us by Olympia. I’ll replace DEI with
merit in government hiring, restore full parental rights in
education, and uphold the biological definition of gender.
Public safety and a classical American education come first.
Kent, Auburn, Covington, and Maple Valley deserve better.
I’ll deliver it.
